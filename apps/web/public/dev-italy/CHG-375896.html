Betting: sportsbook stake, win and odds rules

The stake, maximum-win, system-bet and odds rules that ADM's verification body tests for fixed-odds sports betting, with the payout arithmetic, the dead-heat rule and the limits at a glance.

Use with the task CHG-375896. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails.

At a glance

  • Stakes. Singles and multiples: at least EUR 1.00 in steps of EUR 0.05 (1.05 accepted, 1.03 refused). System bets: at least EUR 2.00 in total, and a multiple of the unit stake Netbet chooses (0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20 or 0.25). Bonus used is part of the stake.
  • Maximum win. A single or multiple that could win more than EUR 50,000 is refused. A system is refused if any one combination could win more than EUR 10,000. If every combination passes but the ticket total exceeds EUR 50,000, a Netbet setting decides: refuse, or accept and cap the payout at EUR 50,000. The player sees the maximum win.
  • Systems. At most 30 events (an event counts once even with several picks). Only the three system kinds ADM defines are sold.
  • Odds and payouts. Decimal odds including the stake, never below 1.00, at most three decimals. Every amount is truncated, never rounded.
  • Dead heat. Odds divided by the number of tied outcomes, cut to three decimals, minimum 1.00, unless the market allows several winners.
  • Settings. Every value is a setting, not hard-coded. Horse racing has its own rules (CHG-375325).

Rules

A. Stakes

  1. Singles and multiples: the ticket amount is at least EUR 1.00 and a multiple of EUR 0.05. The system must not sell a ticket below EUR 1.00 or not a multiple of EUR 0.05. EUR 1.05 is accepted, EUR 1.03 is refused. DM 145/2022 art. 12; Guidelines 3.1.1, 3.1.1.1
  2. System bets: the ticket is at least EUR 2.00. The concessionaire fixes the unit stake between EUR 0.05 and EUR 0.25 in multiples of EUR 0.05; the sale amount must be a multiple of that unit. PSQF carries both the total ticket price (prezzo) and the base stake applied to each generated bet (importo). DD 47010/2009 art. 3 c. 1; Guidelines 3.1.1.1; PSQF 8.2
  3. Bonus used is part of the stake. The minimum and the step apply to the total stake, not to the cash part. Guidelines 3.1.9, 3.1.9.1

B. Maximum win

  1. A single or multiple whose potential win is above EUR 50,000 is not accepted. (To be confirmed, as in the task: whether the calculation is on gross or net winning.) DM 145/2022 art. 14 c. 2; Guidelines 3.1.2
  2. A system: the total win is the sum of the amounts of all winning bets and never above EUR 50,000. A system is not accepted if the potential win of any single bet (combination) is above EUR 10,000; this refusal applies in every setting. DD 47010/2009 art. 4 c. 1 lett. b, art. 5; Guidelines 3.1.2
  3. If the system accepts tickets whose potential win is above EUR 50,000, the payout is limited to EUR 50,000; if it does not accept them, it must not sell them. Which of the two is used is a Netbet setting. A winnings boost counts under the same cap, and PSQF max_pag is the maximum payout including any boost. Guidelines 3.1.2.1; PSQF 8.1; DM 145/2022 art. 10 c. 7
  4. The operator tells the client the minimum and maximum stake and the maximum win allowed, and updates it when ADM changes it. The slip therefore shows the maximum win. Gaming account contract annex 6 art. 6 c. 1

C. System bets

  1. Three kinds are allowed: sistema integrale (several outcomes on the same event, even contrasting, building multiples that contain every selected event); sistema a correzione (n outcomes taken k at a time, with optional fixed outcomes, bankers, in every bet); sistema integrale a correzione (the correction system with several outcomes on the same event). The verification body tests that only these are sold. DD 47010/2009 art. 2 c. 2; Guidelines 3.1.3, 3.1.3.1
  2. A system has at most 30 events. Different bet types may be combined for the events of a system. Systems that mix combination sizes in one ticket (Yankee, Lucky 15 type) are not among the three kinds; whether they may be offered is to be checked with the partner. DD 47010/2009 art. 2 c. 3; Guidelines 3.1.3.1
  3. Protocol limits of a system sale: at most 30 base events, at most 2000 multiples generated by one ticket, request body at most 2000 bytes. Example: all trebles of 30 events are 4060 multiples, so that ticket cannot be sold. A sale of single or multiple bets carries at most 30 bets. PSQF 8.1, 8.2 (7900:6, 7900:7)

D. Odds and payouts

  1. Odds are a number with at most three decimals, include the return of the stake and are never below 1 (1.00 is allowed). In PSQF the sale carries INFO_QUOTA: value 1 means odds in hundredths, value 2 odds in thousandths. DM 145/2022 art. 1 c. 2 lett. r, art. 10 c. 1; Guidelines 3.1.5.1; PSQF 13.24
  2. Single: payout = stake x odds, truncated to two decimals. Example: EUR 3.35 x 2.50 = 8.375, paid EUR 8.37. DM 145/2022 art. 10 c. 3; Guidelines 3.1.6.1
  3. Multiple: the product of the odds is kept to six decimals (truncated), multiplied by the stake, and the payout is truncated to two decimals. Always truncate, never round. Example: 1.50 x 1.50 x 1.50 = 3.375; x EUR 3.35 = 11.30625, paid EUR 11.30. DM 145/2022 art. 10 c. 5; Guidelines 3.1.7.1
  4. Dead heat: the odds are divided by the number of tied outcomes, cut to three decimals, never below 1.00; the new odds are used inside multiples. Where the bet type has several possible winning outcomes, the agreed odds are kept. DM 145/2022 art. 15 c. 1-2; Guidelines 3.1.8, 3.1.8.1
  5. Bonus on fixed-odds bets has three types: full win; reduced win (payout = win minus the bonus used); win in bonus (credited as bonus, bonus used equals the stake). Bonus used is at most the stake and at most EUR 100. No early payment (cash-out) may be offered on a bet that used bonus, even in part. DD 167944/2024 art. 2, 3, 4; DM 145/2022 art. 10 c. 8; Guidelines 3.1.9.1
  6. The odds in force at the sale go into the sale message (INFO_QUOTA is mandatory, each bet carries its own odds). All checks of this task run before the sale is sent, on the odds being sent; after the ticket code the bet cannot be voided. PSQF 8.1, 8.2

E. Settings

  1. Every value is a setting, not hard-coded: minimum stake, step, system minimum, unit stake, the EUR 50,000 and EUR 10,000 limits, refuse-or-cap policy, maximum events, maximum combinations. Horse racing has its own rules and configuration (CHG-375325): fixed-odds minimum unit EUR 3.00 and multiples of EUR 3.00; system unit EUR 0.25 with a ticket minimum of EUR 3.00; odds with two decimals; at most 20 outcomes in a multiple; at most 30 races in a system. Guidelines 3.4.2 to 3.4.6; Det. 61681/RU art. 9, 10

System kinds

KindWhat it is
Sistema integraleSeveral outcomes on the same event, even contrasting. Builds the multiples that contain every selected event.
Sistema a correzionen outcomes taken k at a time. Optional fixed outcomes (bankers) appear in every bet.
Sistema integrale a correzioneThe correction system, with several outcomes allowed on the same event.

All kinds: at most 30 events; different bet types may be combined per event. Examples: full cover, Match A '1' or 'X' with Match B '2' = 2 doubles (A1+B2, AX+B2). Correction, 4 picks taken 2 at a time = 6 doubles; with banker A and picks B, C, D taken 2 at a time = A+B, A+C, A+D. Both together, A '1' or 'X', B '2', C '1' taken 2 events at a time = A1+B2, AX+B2, A1+C1, AX+C1, B2+C1 = 5 doubles.
