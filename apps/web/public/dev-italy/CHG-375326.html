PGDA: casino, card and chance games

ADM's casino protocol (PGDA 3.1.0): game sessions, participation, instant games, results and jackpots, game records, accounting, software hash and signature, with message tables and return codes.

Use with the task CHG-375326. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails. A rehearsal PGDA bench with fake data runs the cases of this kit in the browser.

At a glance

  • Validate first, then move money. A session or table is opened at ADM and accepted before any player joins. A player is registered at ADM and gets a participation code before the stake is debited and before the game starts. If ADM does not answer, the platform resends with the same transaction ID and nothing starts meanwhile.
  • Amount in the session. The player chooses the amount moved from the gaming account into the session: at most EUR 1,000 per session for chance games and cash card games, top-ups and bonus included. The remainder returns to the gaming account when the player leaves. Instant games send one message per participation and have no session.
  • Two-step settlement. The result goes to ADM through PGDA, then the credit goes through PACG (CHG-375322). The balance moves only after both positive replies, within one hour of the official result.
  • Interrupted rounds stay open; no new round in that game until the player finishes it. After 7 days from the start of the user session the round counts as concluded and stays viewable.
  • Jackpots are funded only from games on the same platform and are shown and paid only after ADM confirms them.
  • Records. Every session and round from every provider is stored so a game can be reconstructed for 6 months; session data is kept at least 5 years; ADM has remote access.
  • Switching a game off is allowed only when no session is open in it.
  • Skill games are out of the partner's scope; their messages are listed in section 4 for completeness only.

Rules

A. Session, participation, closing

  1. The platform sends the session opening (400 for RNG and table games, 600 for cash card games). If ADM answers with an error, the platform may not register players and may not send any further message for that session. PGDA 5.4.3, 5.4.4
  2. When a player asks to sit down, the platform sends the participation right purchase (420 or 620) with the amount the player chooses; it is sent again each time the player buys more chips. ADM returns the unique participation code (id_ticket). On an error the platform informs the player and prevents registration and play. PGDA 5.4.3, 5.4.4
  3. The stake is debited and play starts only after ADM has stored the session and returned its code. Guidelines 2.1.1.1
  4. When the player leaves, the platform sends the participation end (430 or 630) with the final stake, the amount the player leaves with, which is transferred to the gaming account. PGDA 5.4.3, 6.4.3
  5. The table closes with 500 (mode 3) or 700 (mode 4). Closing while a player is still seated is refused with error 1431. PGDA 6.4.4, error 1431
  6. Instant games (type 5) use one message, 510, for the participation and any win, and no session. The platform determines the outcome, sends 510, and shows the result and the participation code only after the positive reply. PGDA 5.4.3, 6.4.5
  7. The same player cannot be seated twice at the same table (error 1428); after leaving, a new request to the same table gets a new id_ticket. PGDA 6.4.3, error 1428; Guidelines 2.1.15.1
  8. In a circuit, the proposing concessionaire sends 200, 400 or 600, 250 and 300, 500 or 700; every concessionaire sends 220, 420 or 620 and 430 or 630 for its own customers. PGDA 5.4.5

B. Amounts and limits

  1. The player chooses the amount moved from the gaming account into the session. The limit is EUR 1,000 for the initial stake plus later transfers from the gaming account and any bonus, up to the end of the game session (fixed-odds chance games and non-tournament card games); the participation right in skill games is at most EUR 250. Guidelines 3.7.1, 3.7.1.1, quoting D.D. 666/2011 art. 5
  2. PGDA carries the table limits as session attributes MNI and MXI, in euro cents. A value above the limit allowed by the rules is refused (1491, 1492). A purchase below the table minimum or above the maximum is refused (1432, 1433), and an amount over the playable limit with 1426. PGDA 6.4.1; errors 1426, 1432, 1433, 1491, 1492
  3. The final stake equals the stake taken minus bets plus winnings returned; if the figures do not add up, 430 and 630 are refused (1226). PGDA 6.4.3 fields 4 to 7; error 1226
  4. Every message that moves an account carries the declared balance of the gaming account including the bonus share (declared balance block). PGDA 6.4
  5. Amounts for taxable base are in millionths of a euro (1 cent = 10,000 millionths) and may be negative. PGDA 6.4.3 field 6; 6.7.6 field 7.2

C. Time, repeats, errors

  1. Every transaction (one message plus its reply) has a unique 16-character Id_transazione set by the sender; the reply repeats it. A reply with a different transaction code may be discarded. PGDA 6.1
  2. After a timeout with no reply, the message is sent again without changing it, in particular the transaction code, and the next step depends on the return code received. Retry intervals: 4 seconds for the first three retries, 10 seconds for the fourth and fifth, 30 seconds from the sixth. PGDA 7.2
  3. All systems are synchronised to UTC. Dates in messages are UTC; the acquisition date in replies follows the date in force in Italy. PGDA 4.1, 6.4.2, 6.4.3
  4. The presumed end date given at opening is valid until 24:00 of that day. A session that cannot close by then is extended with 810 sent by the opening concessionaire before the end date passes; no message after the end date is accepted without the extension. PGDA 6.4.1, 5.5 point 6, 6.9.3
  5. A fixed-odds chance or non-tournament card session lasts at most 7 days; a skill game session at most 90 days. Guidelines 2.1.14.1

D. Interrupted rounds and malfunctions

  1. A fixed-odds game must not allow a new round in the same game before the interrupted round is completed; the rule is per game, not per account. After 7 days from the start of the user session the round is treated as concluded and stays viewable by the player. Guidelines 2.1.15.1
  2. For card games played alone, an interrupted hand must be completed before a new hand; after 7 days the hand is concluded with a substitute strategy written in the game rules and documents. The participation then ends with the normal 630. Guidelines 2.1.15.1; PGDA 5.4.4
  3. After a malfunction the platform resumes the round from the interruption point or from the last correctly stored data; otherwise it refunds the stake in full. Guidelines 2.1.15, 2.1.15.1
  4. A table can be closed with 500 or 700 only after a participation end (430 or 630) has been sent for every player seated at it. PGDA error 1431

E. Wins, refunds, jackpots, settlement

  1. Refunds, wins and rectifications are sent to ADM in real time as they occur, following the protocol, and the gaming account moves only after the positive reply. In PGDA the result of a table session travels in 430 or 630 when the player leaves, an instant play in 510, and the detail of each game phase in 580 or 780 every day. Guidelines 2.1.3.1; PGDA 5.5 point 5
  2. Settlement has two steps: (1) the result to ADM through PGDA; (2) the credit to the gaming account through PACG. Credit the account only after the PACG positive reply; winnings are credited within one hour of the official result. Guidelines 2.9.17
  3. Jackpot parts are declared inside the win fields: jackpot internal to the game (attribute JK1) and additional jackpot (JK2), plus the amount allocated to the fund. PGDA 6.4.1, 6.4.3 fields 8, 8a, 20; 6.4.5
  4. A progressive jackpot is built only from play on the same platform. It is shown and paid only if the game rules allow it, after the central system has stored the jackpot. Guidelines 3.7.2.1
  5. 801 communicates the allocation of jackpot funds among the concessionaires that contributed, when the amounts will no longer be awarded in that game. Error 2663: participation in sessions that award jackpot prizes not allowed (420, 510, 620). PGDA 5.5 point 4, 6.9.2; error 2663

F. History, records, ADM access

  1. Game history shows for each game the ADM code and the amount debited, plus the gaming account code and the tax code; the latter two may appear once per page. Guidelines 2.3.2
  2. The code shown to the player is the unique code the central system assigns when it validates the participation right: the id_ticket returned by the first 420 or 620, unchanged through top-ups until the player leaves; for instant games the 510 code. A history line is one stay at the table. The session code is not a display item. Guidelines 2.3.2 to 2.3.4; PGDA 6.4.2
  3. The last-round view shows the ADM code with its timestamp, date and time, result, multipliers and win. Guidelines 2.3.2 to 2.3.4
  4. Stored information of the last 6 months is available in real time and every game of that period can be reconstructed; accounting information for 2 years; everything is kept at least 5 more years with integrity and access. ADM runs queries over user-defined time ranges; results are visible within 48 hours and exportable. Guidelines 2.1.16.1, 2.3.4.1
  5. Reconstruction data per round includes player ID, session and participation right IDs, start time, amounts, jackpot contributions, game state, game progress, wins and end time. Guidelines 2.3.4.1
  6. 570, 575, 770 and 775 are protocol queries for session and participation data; they are separate from ADM's access to the stored records. PGDA 6.7, 6.8; Guidelines 2.1.16.1

G. Switching a game off, accounting, integrity, security

  1. A game application may be disabled only when no gaming activity is open; only temporary disabling is allowed. Removing it is a change to the concessionaire system and needs a new conformity check request. Guidelines 2.4.8.1
  2. Accounting data is requested with 800 for a single day, a month or a year. The reply returns reasons per game type (taxable amount, State tax, tax reversal and the others in table 8.2). A period for which accounting data is not available returns 2499; no accounting data returns 2451. What the platform displays is in CHG-375321. PGDA 6.9.1, 8.2; errors 2451, 2499
  3. 580 and 780 give the phases of a day; once all phases of the day are sent, the balancing 590 or 790 follows. The number of phases in 430 and 630 equals the phases with bets reported in 580 and 780. Only the proposing concessionaire sends 580, 780, 590 and 790. PGDA 5.5 point 5, 6.1, 6.4.3 field 3
  4. 833 sends the hash of every active software component at least daily, and again immediately after patches or fixes. Component types: 1 platform, 2 game, 3 game acceptance system, 4 gaming system. cod_componente is assigned by ADM; the hash is up to 128 characters. PGDA 6.9.4
  5. Messages travel as a binary stream with fixed fields over HTTP POST. Each request and reply is signed in PKCS#7 signed-data with a 2048-bit RSA key and SHA1 digest; the message holds only a certificate reference. A new certificate is added with 840 (base64 X.509) and can sign from 24 hours later; a resend of the same serial replaces the earlier one. PGDA 4.1, 4.2, 6.9.5
  6. Cod_conc_trasmittente and Cod_conc_proponente are ADM-assigned concessionaire codes; Cod_cfs is the code of the service-provider concessionaire. PGDA 6.1 fields 4 to 6
  7. For game sessions in mode 3 BON is B or F; in mode 4 and in skill games BON is B. Error 1470 refuses any other value. B is bonus (counts in turnover, withdrawable once played) and F is fun bonus (wins credited only as bonus); N is used only in the correction messages 561 and 761. PGDA 6.3.1, 6.4.1, 6.5.1; error 1470
  8. The network code of a concessionaire that is a distance-gaming (GAD) concessionaire under art. 24 of law 88/2009 is 14. PGDA 8.5
  9. The player pseudonym is the nickname registered at PACG for the account (message 4.30, 1 to 100 characters); one account per pseudonym (error 1631). It is not a hash or the account code. PACG 4.30, 4.34; Guidelines 3.7.3.1; PGDA error 1631
  10. Game classification: skill 1, fixed-odds chance 2, instant 5, cash card games 3 (between players) and 4 (solitaire). Slots, RNG roulette and RNG table games are type 2 mode 3; instant games type 5; cash card games mode 4. Each game's classification is confirmed with its provider. PGDA 5.3, 5.4, 8.6; Guidelines 2.2.1
