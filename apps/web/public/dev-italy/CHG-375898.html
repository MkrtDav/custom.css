Bonus management and specifications

How bonuses are recorded in the player's account, split by game type, shown to the player and reported to ADM, with ADM's bonus rules for fixed-odds bets and the bonus fields of each protocol.

Use with the task CHG-375898. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails.

At a glance

  • Ledger. Store each bonus award, cancellation and conversion into withdrawable money, and the bonus share of every stake and win. Keep the total bonus and the bonus per game type; the per-game-type amounts add up to the total. Kept for 10 years.
  • Split by game type as ADM's account protocol does, because every movement sent to ADM carries the balance with its bonus share. If the split does not add up to the bonus total, ADM rejects the message.
  • A stake uses only the bonus valid for its game: the amount for that game type, for its family, or for all games; Netbet chooses the split.
  • Player view. Bonus status and wagering requirements are available and easy to find, with progress, not only terms.
  • No withdrawal of bonus. A bonus balance, and winnings credited as bonus, cannot be withdrawn until converted into withdrawable money.
  • Fixed-odds bets: three bonus types, at most EUR 100 of bonus per bet, no cash-out on a bet that used bonus, even in part.
  • The messages that send a bonus award or reversal to ADM (PACG 6.5, 6.6) are in CHG-375322 (section F of its kit); the account ledger is in CHG-375897.

Rules

A. Bonuses in the ledger

  1. For bonuses, store each bonus award, cancellation and conversion into withdrawable money, and the bonus share of every stake and win. Keep the total bonus and the bonus per game type; the per-game-type amounts must add up to the total. Kept for 10 years. Guidelines 2.9.15, 2.9.15.1; Gaming account contract art. 5 c. 20
  2. Split the bonus per game type in the same way ADM's account protocol does (game families such as sports, casino, bingo, or all games), because every movement sent to ADM carries the balance with its bonus share. PACG checks that the type exists (1221), the amount is above zero (1222), there are no duplicate types (1223) and the sum equals the bonus balance (1224). The 6.36 credit allows up to three bonus details. PACG 5, 6.8, 6.36, 7.7, 7.8
  3. A stake can use only the bonus that is valid for its game: the amount for that game type, for its family, or for all games. Netbet chooses and states the split between real money and bonus in the bet message of each betting protocol (PSQF, PSV, PSIP); ADM only checks that the split is valid and adds up. PSQF 8.1; PSV 13.11, 13.12; PSIP 17.53
  4. In PSV and PSIP the bonus is taken from the bonus balance as the BONUS_PREZZO attribute states it; the balance sent with the sale is recalculated after the deduction (example in section 4). PSV 13.11, 13.12; PSIP 17.53, 17.54

B. Bonus status shown to the player

  1. The bonus usage status and the wagering requirements for releasing it are available and easy to find for the player. ADM requires the status of use and the play requirements to be available and easy to find; the itemised view (amount, what is left, wagering requirement, wagered so far, expiry, games it counts on) is Netbet design. Technical rules Part 2 ch. 9; Guidelines 2.9.16, 2.9.16.1; DM 145/2022 art. 11
  2. A bonus balance cannot be withdrawn until it has been converted into withdrawable money. The amounts a player requests for withdrawal cannot include bonus sums or winnings credited as bonus; bonus is used only for play. Guidelines 2.9.16, 2.9.16.1; Technical rules Part 2 ch. 9

C. ADM's rules for bonuses on fixed-odds bets

  1. Three bonus types on fixed-odds bets other than horse races: full win; reduced win (bonus used is deducted from the payment); win paid as bonus (online only). Report the type with the bet and the payment mode. Set the type per bonus product. DD 167944/2024 art. 2; Guidelines 3.1.9, 3.1.9.1; PSQF 14.21
  2. Bonus used per bet: at most 100 euro and never above the stake (equal to the stake for win paid as bonus); a per-bet limit, not on the balance held. Normal minimum stake and maximum possible win apply (CHG-375896). (Should be checked with the partner: whether the 100 euro limit applies to the bonus part of each line in a multiple.) DD 167944/2024 art. 3, art. 4 c. 1; Guidelines 3.1.9.1
  3. No early payment on a bet placed even partly with bonus, partial included (PSQF 5019, CHG-375323). DD 167944/2024 art. 3 c. 3; DM 145/2022 art. 10 c. 8
  4. ADM sets a monthly bonus ceiling per concession and returns it in the accounting message of the betting protocol (PSQF 8000:9 bonus threshold); read it and handle the refusal when exceeded (code 3031). The ceiling is at most 50% of the net movement minus amounts returned in winnings, with a floor of 3% of collection, and 10% of the average in the first two months. PSQF 9.9, 14.30; DD 167944/2024 art. 4 c. 2-4
  5. Publish each bonus's characteristics and conditions: a bonus-terms page (its placement, one click from the bonus area, is Netbet design). DM 145/2022 art. 11; Concession convention art. 15 c. 4 lett. l
  6. Bonus fields follow the connection mode: PSQF has one field pair for connectivity-provider and one for service-provider connections (BONUS_PREZZO and BONUS_PAG_RIMB, and the _2 versions); PSV and PSIP only the service-provider style. All are optional in the sale message. Build the bonus data once in the ledger and map to either set by header profile (CHG-375321). Handle code 3032 (incompatible header version). (Should be checked with the partner: what a direct connection uses; one PSQF payment field is a placeholder in the protocol: confirm with Sogei.) PSQF 13.54, 13.55, 14.21, 14.30

D. ADM's rules for bonuses on casino, skill and card games

  1. A bonus is never withdrawable; winnings obtained by playing with a bonus can be withdrawn. ADM bonus note prot. 2011/20659 of 10 June 2011
  2. A bonus must be played at least once to become game credit (chance games and solitaire card games). There is no direct award of extra bonus inside a game session. ADM bonus note prot. 2011/20659
  3. Credit restrictions tied to a bonus have a predefined duration and the expiry is told to the player in advance; restricted credit is released without restriction if the conditions are not met by expiry. ADM bonus note prot. 2011/20659; D.D. 666/2011 art. 9 c. 1 lett. b n. 6, art. 14 c. 1 lett. k
  4. At the end of a session the final stake is credited split into credit and bonus, and the player is told both amounts. ADM bonus note prot. 2011/20659
  5. Fun bonus: only in dedicated sessions, never mixed with credit or bonus in the initial stake; winnings are paid as bonus (no cash win, no jackpot in cash); same %RTP as the real-money version; never withdrawable; shown as not equal to credit. ADM bonus note prot. 2011/20659
  6. Unused bonus and fun bonus are not counted in the sums that pass to the State after three dormant years. ADM bonus note prot. 2011/20659

Flows

Bonus on a bet

  1. 1
    The platform picks the bonus valid for the bet's game (game type, family or all games) and states the split in the bet message.
  2. 2
    Checks before sending: bonus used is at most the stake and EUR 100; bonus type of the product; no cash-out flag set.
  3. 3
    The sale message carries the price, the bonus part and the balance after the deduction, with the bonus detail by family and type.
  4. 4
    ADM answers 1024 with the ticket ID, or a refusal (3030 invalid bonus, 3031 bonus threshold exceeded, 3032 or 3020 header version not compatible, 1546 bonus not valid in PSIP).
  5. 5
    The ledger posts the bonus part of the stake. At payment the bonus type decides the amount: full win, win minus bonus used, or win credited as bonus.
