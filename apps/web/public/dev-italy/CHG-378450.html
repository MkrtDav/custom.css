AML and fraud: indicators, hold workflow and data for Netbet's AML team

What the law and ADM require on suspicious transactions, alerts and cases, the hold of an operation, UIF requests, retention and staff access.

Use with the task CHG-378450. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails.

At a glance

  • Detection. Systems detect suspicious transactions or flag suspicious cases automatically. ADM names indicator types and periods (day, week, month or longer); neither ADM nor the law gives amounts or counts.
  • Alerts and cases. Each anomaly reaches Netbet's designated function, which assesses it and records the decision. Netbet files reports with UIF itself; this is not a platform integration.
  • Hold. On suspicion the operation is normally not carried out until the report is filed, with a legal exception.
  • UIF requests. Netbet answers UIF requests promptly and keeps the link between a correction and the original report.
  • Confidentiality and funds. The player is never told about a report. Funds of designated persons are frozen and communicated to UIF within thirty days.
  • Half-yearly return. Counts of top-ups and withdrawals over EUR 1,000 and fractioned operations of EUR 15,000 or more in seven days go to ADM every half-year.
  • Staff. Netbet's employees, suppliers and platform developers cannot hold a gaming account.
  • Refusing suspected players is in CHG-376845. Connection data are in CHG-375322 and retention is in CHG-375897.

Rules

A. Indicators and suspected players

  1. Provide systems that detect suspicious transactions or automatically flag suspicious cases. "Suspicious" means Netbet knows, suspects or has reasonable grounds to suspect money laundering or terrorist financing; the law gives no numeric criteria. Concession agreement Art. 15(4)(h); ADM determination 692271 (18 Nov 2024) section 2; D.Lgs. 231/2007 Art. 35(1)
  2. ADM's determination requires indicators for significant movements (top-up and withdrawal amounts per day, week, month or longer, and repetition over time); anomalous concentration of wins or losses in a short period, especially in card games with player interaction (table frequency, users linked by device or IP address); alerts for concentration of top-ups in time bands; and top-up instruments or their combined use. As examples only, ADM also names the ratio of top-ups to withdrawals, different instruments for top-up and withdrawal, and patterns such as a card together with an e-wallet, many cards in a short time, or an instrument not in the player's name; these are not required indicators. ADM gives no amounts or counts. If scratch-card or voucher top-ups are offered, they are used only after a valid ID document and completed due diligence, with indicators on how often they are used against other instruments and their share of top-ups in a period, and Netbet sends ADM adequate information on how the top-up alerts are built. ADM determination 692271 (18 Nov 2024) section 2
  3. (Should be checked with the partner: the criteria and values for these indicators, what counts as significant, unusual, many or short, are not defined by ADM; Netbet's AML function defines and validates them before the build.)
  4. Implement technical measures that reduce money-laundering and fraud risk, and refuse bets, withdrawals and deposits from users suspected of them. Concession agreement Art. 15(4)(f)

B. Alerts and cases

  1. Use the internal suspicious-transaction procedure to escalate anomalies promptly to the designated reporting function. Assess the facts and protect the identities and information involved; keep a trace of the assessment, especially when no report is filed (UIF advises this). Include staff-raised anomalies as well as automatic alerts. UIF instructions of 18 December 2025, Part I §§II-VI and Part II §§I-II
  2. (Should be checked with the partner: whether Netbet keeps its own case tool, fed by export or API, or wants a case queue in the Digitain back office.)

C. Hold of an operation

  1. When the operator knows, suspects or reasonably suspects money laundering, terrorist financing or criminal origin of funds, it sends a report to UIF without delay, including attempted operations. Normally it reports before carrying out the operation and refrains from it until the report is filed. The player is not told why. D.Lgs. 231/2007 Art. 35(1)-(3); UIF instructions of 18 December 2025, Part I §§IV-VI, Part III §§I-II
  2. If the act must legally be received, execution cannot be deferred in normal operations, or deferral may obstruct an investigation, inform UIF immediately after receiving the act or executing the operation. D.Lgs. 231/2007 Art. 35(2)
  3. Respond promptly to UIF information requests. Correct, supplement or replace a filing through the applicable UIF procedure and retain the link to the original report; a replacement report carries the protocol number of the one it replaces, and attachments are limited to documents strictly necessary. An export of a player's account, identity-verification, transaction and game history for a chosen date range is our design choice; the Technical Rules require an operations register with reporting tools, including a list of accounts above a user-set limit over a period. D.Lgs. 231/2007 Art. 35(3); UIF instructions of 18 December 2025, Part II par. II.D and Part III par. II-III; Technical Rules Part 2 ch. 9

D. Data and staff

  1. Identity data and verification results, the date, value and payment instrument of each opening, top-up and withdrawal, and the IP address, date, time and duration of the connections used for them are kept for 10 years from acquisition. Personal data are kept for the contract and 10 years after closing the account. D.Lgs. 231/2007 Art. 53 c. 3; Gaming account contract art. 5 c. 20, art. 19 c. 4
  2. Ensure employees, suppliers and other people involved in developing the operator's game platforms and applications cannot play with the operator or hold a gaming account on its systems. Concession agreement Art. 15(4)(g); D.D. 666/2011 Art. 10(2)

E. Confidentiality, identification and funds

  1. Do not tell the customer or third parties that a report was made, that further information was sent to UIF, or that an investigation exists or is likely. The AML flag and the hold reason are not visible to the player (a consequence of this rule). Communication to the sector authorities and the Guardia di Finanza is not covered by the ban. D.Lgs. 231/2007 Art. 39(1)
  2. When customer due diligence cannot be completed, do not establish, carry out or continue the relationship, and assess whether to file a report. Procedures prevent the payment of winnings when the customer is reluctant to give information or documents, when the documents are unreadable or incomplete, or when the player identified at play differs from the person collecting the winnings. D.Lgs. 231/2007 Art. 42(1); ADM determination 692271 (18 Nov 2024) Art. 2(3)(d)
  3. Funds of persons designated under the counter-terrorist-financing rules are frozen: no transfer, disposal or use. Communicate the measures applied to UIF within thirty days of the date the measure takes effect or, if later, of the date the funds are held (persons in the course of designation are covered, and economic resources are also reported to the Guardia di Finanza special unit); the freeze is effective by law, and Netbet identifies matches and applies it without deciding its timing, with the persons involved and the amount and nature of the funds, and promptly any operations or relationships linked to designated persons. D.Lgs. 109/2007 Art. 5(1), Art. 7(1)-(2)

F. Procedures and the half-yearly return to ADM

  1. Adopt procedures and control systems adequate to mitigate money-laundering and terrorist-financing risks, taking the national risk assessment into account and defining customer risk profiles. Send the description of the procedures to ADM's Direzione Giochi. Adopt an internal channel for staff to report violations, specific, anonymous and independent, protecting the identity of the reporter and of the person reported, and send those procedures to ADM too, in electronic form by PEC to the office managing the concessions, within 30 days of the determination. ADM determination 692271 (18 Nov 2024) Art. 2(1)-(3), (9); D.Lgs. 231/2007 Art. 48
  2. Send the half-yearly return to ADM through its anti-money-laundering telematic service, on a cash basis, zeros included, no later than the end of the month after the half-year closes (31 July for the first half, 31 January for the second). ADM note 2874 of 21 December 2012 §2(b)-(c), §1(d); ADM determination 692271 (18 Nov 2024) Art. 2(8)
  3. Counts for remote gaming: top-ups and withdrawals over EUR 1,000; fractioned operations, meaning several top-ups, or several withdrawals, on the same gaming account within seven consecutive days totalling EUR 15,000 or more, with no minimum per operation for online accounts. The count of each half-year starts on its first day and ends on the last, even if a seven-day window cannot be completed. The seven-day count restarts as the note describes: when EUR 15,000 is reached in fewer than seven days the next count starts the following day; when it is not reached the count restarts from the second day of the window. ADM note 2874 of 21 December 2012 §1(b), (c); ADM note 1586 of 5 July 2012
  4. Non-tournament card games: operations where the sum won in a session (the final stake returned to the gaming account minus the initial stake, including later transfers and bonus) exceeds EUR 1,000, and fractioned wins above EUR 15,000 on the same account in seven consecutive days. Tournament card games: only two-player sessions, fractioned wins net of stakes above EUR 15,000 in seven days. Also the number of suspicious operations communicated to UIF in the half-year. ADM note 1586 of 5 July 2012
  5. (Should be checked with the partner: the dates, boxes and thresholds of the return are from ADM notes of 2012, and who files it for Netbet.)
