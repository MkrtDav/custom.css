Payment methods: Checkout, Pay.com, MyBank, Onshop, LuxonPay, Postepay, TrueLayer

The ADM rules every payment method for deposits and withdrawals follows: traceable instruments in the holder's own name, the payment-method code, the authorisation number and the reversal windows.

Use with the tasks CHG-378451, CHG-378452, CHG-378453, CHG-378454, CHG-378455, CHG-378456, CHG-378457. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails.

At a glance

  • Own name only. Only traceable payment instruments in the holder's own name are accepted; withdrawals go only to an instrument of the holder. The exception is a top-up at a point of sale (PVR), which may be in cash or another instrument up to EUR 100 per week, Monday to Sunday, and the system blocks going above it (codes 1495, 1496), if PVR top-ups are offered.
  • Method code. Each deposit and withdrawal carries ADM's payment-method code (table 7.9). Which code each method uses is to be checked with the partner.
  • Authorisation number. The provider's authorisation number is logged for every transaction.
  • Reversals. A normal reversal within 15 days, a charge-back within 196 days.
  • The tickets: CHG-378451 Checkout (Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay), CHG-378452 Pay.com, CHG-378453 MyBank, CHG-378454 Onshop, CHG-378455 LuxonPay, CHG-378456 Postepay, CHG-378457 TrueLayer. Account-register rules are in CHG-375322.

Rules

  1. Only traceable payment instruments in the holder's own name are accepted, with the exception of top-ups at a PVR up to EUR 100 per week (PACG codes 1495 and 1496). All top-up instruments must be issued by entities subject to the EU anti-money-laundering directives or to equivalent rules. Guidelines 2.9.3, 2.9.3.1; Concession agreement Art. 15(4)(i), 17(3)(b); ADM determination 675265, Art. 2(1), 3(1)(a), 5(1); ADM determination 692271 Annex par. 2; Technical rules Part 2 ch. 9
  2. Each deposit and each withdrawal carries the PACG payment-method code (table 7.9); every platform or provider method maps to one code, and a code not allowed for the movement is refused (1421). (Should be checked with the partner: the code of each method above.) PACG table 7.9, code 1421
  3. A withdrawal is recorded first like a deposit and goes only to a payment account or instrument in the holder's own name among bank transfer, direct debit, payment card, prepaid card and other services of an Italian or EU payment provider that identify payer and payee. The concessionaire checks that the instrument belongs to the holder and refuses otherwise: if the instrument is not in the holder's name, do not pay, tell the holder and ask for a suitable instrument. A withdrawal is followed by PACG 6.33 (post-withdrawal payment, the date the debit reached the concessionaire's account; not sent if a withdrawal reversal was already sent); 6.34 reverses it, only after a 6.33; and a withdrawal reversal (6.4) is sent before the 6.33 or after its reversal. The accounting evidence of top-ups with ordinary instruments is kept separate from the special instruments and from cash (determination 675265 Art. 4(2)). ADM determination 675265/RU of 22 October 2026 art. 2(1), 5(1) to 5(4); PACG 6.3 table 7.9
  4. The system keeps a log of all authorisation numbers given by the payment service providers when approving a transaction. Guidelines 2.9.20, 2.9.20.1 (top-up transactions); Technical rules Part 2 ch. 9
  5. Reversal (PACG 6.4): a normal reversal within 15 days, a charge-back reversal within 196 days (28 weeks); later ones are refused (1239). PACG 6.4, code 1239
  6. A deposit is refused when it would exceed the deposit self-limit (1494) and on closed (1203), dormant (1204), blocked (1271) and suspended accounts with reason 9, 10 or 11 (1471). PACG 6.3
  7. For an already-used payment instrument meeting Art. 3(1) of ADM's determination, the concessionaire verifies the requirements and submits the issuer's technical report, attested by an independent AML and/or banking expert, to ADM by 31 March 2027. It applies when Netbet identifies such an instrument. ADM determination 675265, Art. 3(1), (2), (5)
