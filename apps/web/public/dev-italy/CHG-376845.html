Responsible gaming: player risk monitoring and operator-set limits

What ADM requires for detecting players at risk, the measures that follow, and the technical measures against suspected players on every game.

Use with the task CHG-376845. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails.

At a glance

  • Detection. Predictive behavioural analytics and risk indicators identify players at risk of problem gambling. ADM names no signals, values or criteria.
  • Measures. When risk indices are exceeded, the operator applies restrictive measures such as spending, time and deposit limits.
  • Player contact. Systems for interacting with players showing possible risk, and prevention contact channels open at least five days a week and eight hours a day.
  • Suspected players. Technical measures reduce collusion, money-laundering and fraud risk and refuse bets, deposits and withdrawals of suspected players, on every game.
  • Session time and spending alerts are in CHG-375442. AML and fraud are in CHG-378450.

Rules

A. Detection and measures

  1. Use predictive behavioural analytics and risk indicators to identify players at risk of problem gambling; use behavioural science and advanced technology, including AI tools, to analyse gaming-habit data and prevent problem gambling. ADM names no signals, values or criteria. (Should be checked with the partner: whether rule-based indicators alone are accepted, since ADM's wording says predictive analysis.) Administrative Rules §7.3.1(d)(i)-(ii), (vi); Concession agreement Art. 20(3)(a), (b), (f); D.Lgs. 41/2024 Art. 14(2)
  2. Provide tools that give the operator closer control over the gaming participation of players most at risk, in line with data protection law. When risk indices are exceeded, apply restrictive measures such as spending, time and deposit limits imposed by the operator. Administrative Rules §7.3.1(d)(iv); Concession agreement Art. 20(1)(h), (3)(d); D.Lgs. 41/2024 Art. 15(1)(h)
  3. Operator-imposed limits apply per player when that player exceeds risk indices, not as one limit for the whole site. They are separate from the player's own limits (the stricter applies), and the player is told at least 7 days ahead. Guidelines 2.9.1.1; PACG 6.23, table 7.18
  4. Develop systems for interacting with players showing possible problem-gambling risk. Provide responsible-gaming information and prevention contact channels operating continuously for at least five days a week and eight hours a day, and train the contact-centre staff. Administrative Rules §7.3.1(d)(v); Concession agreement Art. 20(1)(f), (3)(e)

B. Suspected players, every game

  1. Implement technical measures that reduce collusion, money-laundering and fraud risk, and refuse bets, withdrawals and deposits from users suspected of them. Concession agreement Art. 15(4)(f)
  2. ADM's example patterns: in non-tournament card games, the same two users at two-player tables in at least three sessions with one user winning over EUR 5,000; in fixed-odds sports and horse betting, a new account depositing and staking over EUR 1,000 then immediately asking to withdraw; or, under high exposure from anomalous flows, users placing within 120 seconds bets on the same bet model with an identical outcome, or on different models with outcomes tied to a predetermined result. Concession agreement Art. 15(4)(f)
  3. Provide systems that detect suspicious transactions or automatically flag suspicious cases, and prevent two users with the same IP address from joining the same non-tournament card game between players (needed only if such games are offered). Concession agreement Art. 15(4)(h); ADM determination 692271 (18 Nov 2024) section 2
  4. (Should be checked with the partner: ADM does not define "suspected", "abnormal bet flows" or "high exposure"; the criteria and values are validated with Netbet.)
