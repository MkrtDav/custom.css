Netbet Italy · developer kit · CHG-375442

Responsible gaming: session time and spending alerts

The non-blocking alert shown during the user session when the time or spending threshold is reached, its log, and the responsible-gaming information shown at the point of bet.

Use with the task CHG-375442. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails.

1. At a glance

  • Alerts. During the user session the player sees an alert whenever the spending threshold and/or the time threshold is reached. Spending: at most EUR 100 per session. Time: at most one hour of connection time.
  • Ceilings. EUR 100 and one hour are ceilings. Lower values must be possible for some groups, so thresholds are configurable per age band and per player.
  • Repeat. The alert comes again at each next threshold: at minimum every hour and every EUR 100.
  • Never blocks. Blocking comes only from other tools: the player's limits and the limits Netbet imposes (CHG-375322), self-exclusion, and the inactivity time-out (CHG-375441).
  • Log. Every alert is logged: player, session, device, threshold, time shown, acknowledgement.
  • Point of bet. Responsible-gaming information (addiction risk, 18+, link to the responsible-gaming area and to the player's limits) is shown in the bet slip and at game launch, with a slot for ADM messages.
  • This task has no ADM message of its own.

What the platform builds

  • Server-side counters per user session: connection time since login (any device) and session spending (staked minus won and refunded).
  • Threshold configuration per age band and per player, never above EUR 100 and one hour. The risk signals and logic are in a separate task.
  • A real-time, non-blocking push of the alert to every device where the user session is active, carrying time played and spending.
  • Repetition at each next threshold; no code path may reject a bet because an alert was shown or ignored.
  • An alert log with the fields in section 4, kept with the other stored data that ADM can query.
  • The responsible-gaming information component in the bet slip and at game launch, with a CMS slot.

2. Rules

A. Thresholds and counters

  1. Show alerts during the user session whenever the spending threshold and/or the time threshold is reached. The two thresholds may be used together or alone. Guidelines 2.9.2; ADM FAQ 12 March 2025 no. 39
  2. Spending threshold: at most EUR 100 per user session. Spending is the difference between the amounts played and the amounts won and refunded to the player. Guidelines 2.9.2.1
  3. Played amounts are the amounts used to make a bet, or transferred from the gaming account to join a game session. Won and refunded amounts are the amounts credited to the gaming account for wins and refunds, or at the end of a game session. In casino table games the amount brought to the table is PGDA message 420 and the amount returned is message 430. Guidelines 2.9.2.1; PGDA 5.4.3
  4. Time threshold: at most one hour. Time is the connection time, the interval between the player's login to the gaming account and the disconnection, regardless of the device used to log in. Two devices connected at the same time do not add up. Guidelines 2.9.2.1; ADM FAQ 17 April 2025 no. 28
  5. Connection time has no pause for idle time or a background tab. The clock stops only when the user session ends. Guidelines 2.9.2.1; ADM FAQ 12 March 2025 no. 38
  6. The user session starts with the player's login. It ends when the player ends it, when the inactivity time-out is exceeded, when a documented condition set by the concessionaire requires it, or on forced closure of the site or app (CHG-375441). ADM FAQ 12 March 2025 no. 38; ADM FAQ 17 April 2025 no. 28; Technical rules Part 2 ch. 1
  7. The thresholds are set by the concessionaire according to the player's age and gaming habits. EUR 100 and one hour are ceilings; lower values must be possible for some groups. Build them configurable per age band and per player, not as one global value. ADM gives no values per age. Guidelines 2.9.2, 2.9.2.1
  8. The age clusters used for self-limits (18 to 24 and over 25) may be one criterion for the thresholds. They are not mandatory and not the only criterion. ADM FAQ 12 March 2025 no. 40
  9. The alert is shown every time the thresholds are reached, so it repeats: at minimum every hour of continuous play and every EUR 100 spent in the session. With the maximum values, a player online for 2 h 30 gets at least 2 time alerts (at 1 h and 2 h); a session with EUR 201 of spending gets at least 2 spending alerts (at EUR 100 and EUR 200). The player can keep playing. Guidelines 2.9.2
  10. Spending is a difference, so it can be negative when the player is ahead. A session in which the player is net ahead does not reach a spending threshold. Guidelines 2.9.2.1

B. How the alert is shown

  1. The alert is shown on every device where the player's user session is active (web and app at the same time), at least when the time or spending threshold is exceeded, without preventing normal play. Guidelines 2.9.2.1
  2. The alert is a real-time, non-blocking popup or banner pushed to all active sessions (for example through the existing websocket). It shows the time played and the spending in the session. The player closes it and keeps playing. Concession convention art. 20 c. 1 lett. c
  3. An error message on the next transaction is not an alert. It blocks an action and comes late; that is the behaviour of a limit breach, not of an alert.
  4. The site and the apps are in Italian (technical terms of common use excepted), so the alert text is in Italian. Guidelines 2.7.7

C. What blocks and what does not

  1. Alerts block nothing. Blocking comes only from other tools: the player's own limits and operator-imposed limits (CHG-375322), self-exclusion, and the inactivity time-out (CHG-375441).
  2. When a limit set by the player is exceeded, the concessionaire's system stops all gaming activity at once in every active user session, keeps access to the gaming account, and communicates the breach to the central system (PACG 6.25 Self-limit exceeded). The alert is a separate, non-blocking message. Guidelines 2.9.1.1, 2.9.2.1; PACG 6.25
  3. ADM requires only the alert itself. Further actions belong to other rules: restrictions when risk indices are exceeded, and Netbet's own steps (break invitation, support links, review by the responsible-gaming team). The protocol communication to the central system in this area concerns the breach of a player limit, not alerts.

D. Logging

  1. Log every alert: player, session, device, threshold reached, time shown, and the player's acknowledgement. Netbet's responsible-gaming policy uses ignored alerts as a risk signal (the risk signals and logic are in a separate task).
  2. The information stored by the concessionaire must be available to ADM in real time for the last six months (accounting information for the last two years). Technical rules Part 2 ch. 1; ADM FAQ 12 March 2025 no. 41

E. Responsible-gaming information at the point of bet

  1. When the player is about to place a bet, show the responsible-gaming and player-protection information in the bet slip and at game launch: addiction risk, 18+, a link to the responsible-gaming area and to the player's limits, plus a CMS slot for ADM messages.
  2. ADM gives no format for this information. It need not be a popup before each bet; a footer or header also works if it is visible in the betting view (not hidden, scrolled away or covered).
  3. Site and apps must show, clearly visible, the ban on play for minors and notices or links with at least: information on the potential risks of gambling and contacts for help; a list of player-protection measures with the possibility to use them; a link to the ADM site; a way to lodge complaints with a link to the ADM reports portal. Guidelines 2.7.4, 2.7.4.1

3. Flows

Alert sequence with web and app active

StepWhat happens
1Login: the user session starts; the player also logs in on the app.
2Bets, wins and refunds update the counters: connection time since login (any device) and session spending (staked minus won minus refunded).
3A time or spending threshold is reached.
4The alert is pushed in real time to every device with an active session. It shows time played and session spending without blocking.
5The player closes the alert (acknowledgement).
6One log row per alert and device: player, session, device, threshold reached, time shown, acknowledgement.
7The next threshold is reached (second hour, next EUR 100): the alert is shown again on every active device.

Evaluation

Next threshold reached? (time: each hour or lower value; spending: each EUR 100 or lower value)No: no alert, play continues. Yes: an alert on every device with an active user session, closable, never blocking, and one log row per alert and device.

A limit breach (own limit, operator limit, self-exclusion, inactivity time-out) is a different message and does block play. It is not an alert.

6. Screens

  • Alert: a popup or banner that does not block play: time played, spending in the session, and a way to close it. It must not be confused with a limit-breach message, which is a stop screen. The same alert on web and on the app when both are active. On mobile the banner never covers the Place bet button. All texts in Italian.
  • Responsible-gaming strip in the bet slip and at game launch: 18+, addiction risk, link to the responsible-gaming area and to the player's limits; visible, not hidden, scrolled away or covered. A footer is accepted.
