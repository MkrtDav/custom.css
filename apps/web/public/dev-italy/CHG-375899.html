Two-factor login, sub-registration and login security

The Italian login flow end to end: credentials, second factor, the ADM account-register check (PACG 6.12), the disconnection message (6.32), the previous-login display and protection against unauthorised access.

Use with the task CHG-375899. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails.

1. At a glance

  • Three steps, in this order: credentials, a second factor of Netbet's choice, the sub-registration request to ADM's central system (PACG 6.12). The session starts and the gaming area opens only after the positive reply.
  • Two-factor login is the only way in. No technical cookie authenticates the player: no "stay logged in" or "remember me" that skips a step. Every new session repeats the whole chain.
  • Sub-registration is sent at every login, immediately after the second factor, with the balance and its bonus share. It checks the account status and any self-exclusion.
  • Receipt and logout. The receipt ID from 6.12 is stored with the session and quoted in the disconnection message 6.32 (CHG-375441).
  • Previous login is shown after login in hours, minutes and seconds, Italian time.
  • Unauthorised access is detected and the player is warned at once. Three consecutive failed logins (including a failed second factor) lock the account temporarily and the player is notified at once by e-mail or push.

What the platform builds

  • An authentication service with a server-side login flow: password, second factor, then the sub-registration; a factor registry per account (SMS, e-mail, authenticator, biometrics in the app); a factor becomes active only after the player proves possession at enrolment.
  • A session service that creates the session only on 1024 and stores the receipt ID with it; no token is issued before; the only cookie is the session token, which ends with the session.
  • PACG adapter calls for 6.12 (declared balance and bonus share read from the ledger at that moment, new transaction ID) and 6.32 through an outbox with same-ID retry. The adapter is the one of CHG-375322, with message security from CHG-375321.
  • A failure counter and lock per account (failed password or second factor), reset at success, with the immediate e-mail or push notification.
  • Previous login: read the previous completed session before writing the new one and show it in Italian time.
  • A security event log with a SIEM forwarder and a rule that alerts on frequent security-setting changes.

2. Rules

A. Second factor and cookies

  1. Two-factor login: after the credentials the player gives a second factor of Netbet's choice (SMS or e-mail one-time code, authenticator app, biometrics in the app), enrolled at registration and asked at every login. No "remember me" cookie that skips any step. A "trusted device" shortcut that skips the second factor is not allowed.
  2. ADM requires multi-factor authentication as the only mechanism of access to gaming accounts: after the credentials the system asks for a second verification by a method chosen by the concessionaire, and a user session opens only after it. Guidelines 2.9.10, 2.9.10.1; Technical rules Part 2 ch. 9
  3. Technical cookies may not be used to authenticate the user. A session cookie never bypasses a step: every new session repeats credentials, second factor and the sub-registration. Guidelines 2.9.10.1

B. Sub-registration at login (6.12)

  1. After the second factor the platform sends the sub-registration request (PACG 6.12) at once, with the declared balance and its bonus share. 6.12 checks the account state and any self-exclusion. Guidelines 2.9.10.1; PACG 6.12
  2. The user session starts and the gaming area opens only after the positive reply (1024). Without a reply, or with any other code, no session exists. Guidelines 2.9.10.1
  3. Every session has its own sub-registration, its own transaction ID and its own receipt ID. ADM does not require a single active session: time limits count login to disconnection on any device, and each session needs its own 6.12. Guidelines 2.9.10.1, 2.9.1.1; ADM FAQ 17 April 2025
  4. The refusals handled at login are 1487 account suspended, 1490 blocked, 1488 closed, 1489 dormant, 1452 cross-operator self-exclusion and 1491 self-exclusion from one or more game families. A dormant account (three years without movement) may not be accessed. Balance and bonus-detail errors (1217, 1218, 1220 to 1224) and the technical codes are not shown to the player as account refusals. PACG 6.12 return codes; Guidelines 2.9.14
  5. For a category self-exclusion (1491) gaming and bonuses are barred only for the selected categories, and the self-excluded player must be able to enter the account for management. Guidelines 2.9.4.1
  6. For a suspension the player is told the reason, except when the judicial authority or ADM asks for confidentiality of an investigation. For a block the reasons are kept in a register. Gaming account contract art. 14 para 2; Guidelines 2.9.5, 2.9.5.1
  7. When no reply arrives within the platform's time-out the request is resent unchanged, at 4 seconds for the first three retries, 10 seconds for the fourth and fifth, and 30 seconds from the sixth. The platform acts on the return code received. 1025 (temporarily prohibited) and 1026 (still being processed) are common return codes. PACG 2.1, C1 list
  8. The declared balance is read from the ledger at the moment of the request; ADM requires the balance with its bonus share in every movement and in the sub-registration. After 1027 the reply does not repeat the original result. Guidelines 2.9.8, 2.9.8.1; PACG 6.12, 2.1
  9. (Should be checked with the partner: the rule says access to gaming depends on sub-registration once ADM has adopted the relevant provisions and in the manner it sets. Build it as described and confirm with Sogei at the connection tests.)

C. Receipt and disconnection (6.32)

  1. The receipt ID (ID Ricevuta, 25 characters in the 6.32 request) returned by 6.12 is stored with the session. At logout the platform sends the disconnection message 6.32 with that receipt ID and the current balance and bonus share. PACG 6.12, 6.32
  2. A failed 6.32 is retried with the same transaction ID. 1463 (disconnection already sent) means the item is done; 1464 (receipt ID missing or inconsistent) and 1465 (account differs from the sub-registration account) are real errors. PACG 6.32 return codes; PACG 2.1
  3. A user session ends when the player logs out, when the time-out is reached, when documented operator conditions require it, or when the site or app is closed by force. The default idle time-out is not above 20 minutes and never above 60. Session ends and time-outs are in CHG-375441. ADM FAQ 17 April 2025; Guidelines 2.1.14.1

D. Previous login and time

  1. After login the player sees the date and time of the previous session in hours, minutes and seconds, Italian time; make sure seconds are shown. The check is on the date and time of the last user session opened by the player; end time and failed attempts are not required. The feature already exists for other markets and can be reused. Guidelines 2.9.11, 2.9.11.1
  2. All components of the concessionaire's system use UTC as the time reference, and logs and timestamps of messages refer to a UTC source. PACG messages carry times in CET with automatic daylight-saving handling. The platform stores UTC, converts at the PACG boundary and shows Italian time to the player. Guidelines 2.1.5, 2.1.5.1; PACG 3

E. Unauthorised access and security events

  1. The platform detects suspicious login attempts and warns the player at once. ADM requires that the player is told whenever the concessionaire believes an unauthorised access attempt took place, in the way best suited to prevent fraudulent use, and that the system can detect such attempts and has a mechanism to tell the player. The channel and wording are the operator's choice, and ADM sets no list of cases. Guidelines 2.9.12, 2.9.12.1
  2. Three consecutive failed logins, including a failed second factor, lock the account temporarily and the player is notified at once by e-mail or push. The lock exists today; the notification is to build.
  3. Every security event (failed login, lock, password change, e-mail change, second-factor change) is logged and forwarded to the SIEM, with an alert on frequent security-setting changes.
  4. Logs and timestamps of the messages exchanged with the central system are kept. Real-time access to all stored information is kept for 6 months, accounting data for 2 years, then at least 5 more years with integrity, readability and access. Analytic detail of movements is kept 10 years and personal data for the contract term plus 10 years after closure. Guidelines 2.1.16, 2.1.5.1; Gaming account contract art. 5 para 20, art. 19 para 4

5. Acceptance checklist

CasePasses when
EnrolmentA new player enrols a second factor and proves possession (code or biometric prompt) before activation; SMS, authenticator, e-mail and biometrics can be enrolled; biometrics is absent on web.
Second factorAfter a correct password a second-factor step follows every time on every channel and its success is required to continue.
Old cookieA session that ended, or a second tab or browser with an old cookie, opens nothing; credentials, second factor and 6.12 are repeated.
6.12 contentAfter a correct second factor 6.12 is sent with a new transaction ID, the account code, the declared balance and the bonus share in cents.

LockThree consecutive failures (password or second factor) lock the account temporarily, further attempts are refused, and the player is notified at once by e-mail or push; success or the end of the lock resets the counter.

6. Screens

  • Enrolment at registration: method choice (biometrics only in the app), proof of possession, then continue activation. States: code sent, code entry, wrong code, expired code, verified; authenticator QR and manual key; biometric prompt success, failure, cancelled, not available.
  • Login: credentials; the second-factor screen for the enrolled method (code entry, wrong or expired code, resend, use another method); a "checking your account" state during 6.12, with a service-unavailable state after the platform's maximum wait; then the gaming area with the previous-login line (hh:mm:ss, Italian time), or a first-access message.
  • Refusal pages: one message per case: suspended, blocked, closed, dormant, self-excluded across operators, self-excluded from categories.
  • Lock screen and the lock e-mail or push notification.
  • Security settings: change password, e-mail or second factor, each with a confirmation and a notice that an alert was sent.
