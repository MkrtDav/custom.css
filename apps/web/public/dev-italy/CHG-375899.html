Two-factor login, sub-registration and login security

The Italian login flow end to end: credentials, second factor, the ADM account-register check (PACG 6.12), the disconnection message (6.32), the previous-login display and protection against unauthorised access.

Use with the task CHG-375899. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails.

At a glance

  • Three steps, in this order: credentials, a second factor of Netbet's choice, the sub-registration request to ADM's central system (PACG 6.12). The session starts and the gaming area opens only after the positive reply.
  • Two-factor login is the only way in. No technical cookie authenticates the player: no "stay logged in" or "remember me" that skips a step. Every new session repeats the whole chain.
  • Sub-registration is sent at every login, immediately after the second factor, with the balance and its bonus share. It checks the account status and any self-exclusion.
  • Receipt and logout. The receipt ID from 6.12 is stored with the session and quoted in the disconnection message 6.32 (CHG-375441).
  • Previous login is shown after login in hours, minutes and seconds.
  • Unauthorised access is detected and the player is warned at once. A lock after failed logins, and how the player is notified, are Netbet design, not an ADM rule: ADM requires only that attempts are detected and the player told in the way the concessionaire finds best.

Rules

A. Second factor and cookies

  1. ADM requires multi-factor authentication as the only mechanism of access to gaming accounts: after the credentials the system asks for a second verification by a method chosen by the concessionaire, and a user session opens only after it. Guidelines 2.9.10, 2.9.10.1; Technical rules Part 2 ch. 9
  2. Technical cookies may not be used to authenticate the user. A session cookie never bypasses a step: every new session repeats credentials, second factor and the sub-registration. Guidelines 2.9.10.1

B. Sub-registration at login (6.12)

  1. After the second factor the platform sends the sub-registration request (PACG 6.12) at once, with the declared balance and its bonus share. 6.12 checks the account state and any self-exclusion. Guidelines 2.9.10.1; PACG 6.12
  2. The user session starts and the gaming area opens only after the positive reply (1024). Without a reply, or with any other code, no gaming session exists. Guidelines 2.9.10.1
  3. Every session has its own sub-registration, its own transaction ID and its own receipt ID. Time limits count login to disconnection on any device, and each session needs its own 6.12. Guidelines 2.9.10.1, 2.9.1.1; ADM FAQ 17 April 2025
  4. The refusals handled at login are 1487 account suspended, 1490 blocked, 1488 closed, 1489 dormant, 1452 cross-operator self-exclusion and 1491 self-exclusion from one or more game families. A dormant account (three years without movement) may not be accessed. Balance and bonus-detail errors (1217, 1218, 1220 to 1224) and the technical codes are not shown to the player as account refusals. PACG 6.12 return codes; Guidelines 2.9.14
  5. For a category self-exclusion (1491) gaming and bonuses are barred only for the selected categories, and the self-excluded player must be able to enter the account for management. Guidelines 2.9.4.1
  6. For a suspension the player is told the reason, except when the judicial authority or ADM asks for confidentiality of an investigation. For a block the reasons are kept in a register. Gaming account contract art. 14 para 2; Guidelines 2.9.5, 2.9.5.1
  7. When no reply arrives within the platform's time-out the request is resent unchanged, at 4 seconds for the first three retries, 10 seconds for the fourth and fifth, and 30 seconds from the sixth. The platform acts on the return code received. 1025 (temporarily prohibited) and 1026 (still being processed) are common return codes; PACG 2.1 prescribes the resend only after a time-out with no reply, so what to do on 1025 and 1026 is the platform's choice. PACG 2.1; return-code lists in chapter 6
  8. The declared balance is read from the ledger at the moment of the request; ADM requires the balance with its bonus share in every movement and in the sub-registration. After 1027 the reply does not repeat the original result. Guidelines 2.9.8, 2.9.8.1; PACG 6.12, 2.1

C. Receipt and disconnection (6.32)

  1. The receipt ID (ID Ricevuta, 25 characters in the 6.32 request) returned by 6.12 is stored with the session. At logout the platform sends the disconnection message 6.32 with that receipt ID and the current balance and bonus share. PACG 6.12, 6.32
  2. A failed 6.32 is retried with the same transaction ID. 1463 (disconnection already sent) means the item is done; 1464 (receipt ID missing or inconsistent) and 1465 (account differs from the sub-registration account) are real errors. PACG 6.32 return codes; PACG 2.1
  3. A user session ends when the player logs out, when the time-out is reached, when documented operator conditions require it, or when the site or app is closed by force. The default idle time-out is not above 20 minutes, and a player-set value is below 60. Session ends and time-outs are in CHG-375441. ADM FAQ 17 April 2025; Guidelines 2.1.14.1

D. Previous login and time

  1. After login the player sees the date and time of the previous session in hours, minutes and seconds, make sure seconds are shown. The check is on the date and time of the last user session opened by the player; end time and failed attempts are not required. Guidelines 2.9.11, 2.9.11.1
  2. All components of the concessionaire's system use UTC as the time reference, and logs and timestamps of messages refer to a UTC source. The V3 PACG response timestamps are UTC; request fields typed Ora or DataOra are CET with automatic daylight-saving handling, so follow the field type. The platform stores UTC and converts at the PACG boundary. Guidelines 2.1.5, 2.1.5.1; PACG 5 (pp. 164-165)

E. Unauthorised access and security events

  1. The platform detects suspicious login attempts and warns the player at once. ADM requires that the player is told whenever the concessionaire believes an unauthorised access attempt took place, in the way best suited to prevent fraudulent use, and that the system can detect such attempts and has a mechanism to tell the player. The channel and wording are the operator's choice, and ADM sets no list of cases. Guidelines 2.9.12, 2.9.12.1
  2. Logs and timestamps of the messages exchanged with the central system are kept. Real-time access to all stored information is kept for 6 months, accounting data for 2 years, then at least 5 more years with integrity, readability and access. Analytic detail of movements is kept 10 years and personal data for the contract term plus 10 years after closure. Guidelines 2.1.16, 2.1.5.1; Gaming account contract art. 5 para 20, art. 19 para 4
