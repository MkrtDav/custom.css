Netbet Italy · developer kit · CHG-376993

Player account states and their management

The account states ADM defines, who can set them, what each state allows and how it ends, the changes Netbet and ADM make, the player's requests and the dormant-account process.

Use with the task CHG-376993. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails.

1. At a glance

  • Five states (PACG table 7.2): open, suspended, closed, dormant, blocked.
  • Netbet sends a state change to ADM at the moment of the action; the state changes only after ADM's positive reply. The player and ADM are told the reason, unless confidentiality is requested.
  • ADM states are picked up at every login through the sub-registration. Netbet never reopens an account that ADM suspended or blocked.
  • Suspension lasts at most 180 days. A block comes only after a suspension and only ADM lifts it. A closed account is never reopened.
  • Dormant: three years without movements; access blocked, the account reported to ADM, the balance (without bonus) passes to the State.
  • The PACG messages and return codes are in the kit of CHG-375322 (sections K and L); the ledger is in CHG-375897.

What the platform builds

  • An account state machine that rejects the transitions 6.10 would refuse, with the reasons and conditions below.
  • Back-office actions with a mandatory reason from a fixed list mapped to the PACG reason codes, a free-text note, and an electronic register of user and time.
  • The player's own requests: time-out, close account, expired-document suspension.
  • A daily dormancy job: finds accounts three years after the last movement, skips accounts with undecided bets, blocks access, sends PACG 6.14, writes the per-account report row.

2. Rules

A. Account states

  1. The states and what each allows: see the table below. PACG 7.2; Gaming account contract art. 14, 15, 16
  2. A suspension lasts at most 180 days, then the account returns to open unless blocked or closed. Concession agreement art. 17 c. 3 (a); Gaming account contract art. 14; Guidelines 2.9.5.1
  3. Withdrawals are allowed by default during a suspension unless the reason forbids it (fraud or collusion investigations, ADM or court orders, expired ID document), and never in the blocked state. The central system refuses movements on accounts suspended with reasons 9, 10, 11 (1471) and on blocked accounts (1271). On a closed account a withdrawal is allowed unless the reason is 9, 10 or 11; a top-up is refused (1203). PACG 6.3 description and codes

B. Changes made by Netbet

  1. Back office: suspend or block only on the grounds of the gaming account contract (fraud, collusion, use by others; Netbet's initiative or an ADM or court request for transparency, security, proper service or force majeure). Mandatory reason from a fixed list mapped to the ADM reason codes, free-text note, user and time stored in an electronic register. Gaming account contract art. 14; Guidelines 2.9.5, 2.9.5.1
  2. Send the state change to ADM at the moment of the action (PACG 6.10); the state changes only after ADM's positive reply. Tell the player and ADM the reason, unless confidentiality is requested. PACG 6.10; Guidelines 2.9.5.1
  3. Reasons Netbet can send: 2 requested by Netbet, 3 requested by the holder, 6 documents not sent, 7 suspected fraud, collusion or use by third parties, 10 on behalf of the judicial authority. Reasons only ADM sets and Netbet cannot send: 1 requested by ADM, 5 death of the holder, 9 document expired, 11 ADM on behalf of the judicial authority. Self-exclusion (reason 8) has its own messages, see CHG-375322. PACG 7.2, 7.3, 6.10
  4. 6.10 refusals the platform pre-empts: 1208 to 1210 reopen from open, closed, dormant; 1211 to 1213 and 1507 suspend; 1214, 1215 close; 1275, 1276, 1505, 1506 block (a block follows a suspension); 1234 invalid reason; 1514 legal-entity account. PACG 6.10 return codes
  5. Netbet can ask ADM for the current state of an account at any time (status query, PACG 6.11). PACG 6.11

C. Changes made by ADM

  1. ADM states are picked up at every login through the sub-registration request. The session is refused for suspended (1487), blocked (1490), closed (1488), dormant (1489) and self-excluded accounts (1452, 1491). Accounts are not polled during the day; a refused operation in a session (1271, 1471) ends the session. PACG 6.12, 6.3
  2. Netbet never reopens an account that ADM suspended or blocked. When ADM lifts a state, the next login check returns OK.

D. Requests from the player

  1. Time-out: the player suspends their own account (reason 3); the rules for starting and ending the pause are published on the site.
  2. Close account: self-service in the profile, sent to ADM at once (reason 3), no approval step; withdrawing from the contract means closing the account. The player may also withdraw from the contract by registered letter or digitally signed or certified e-mail; it counts from the day it is sent, and support closes the account. Guidelines 2.9.13, 2.9.13.1; Gaming account contract
  3. Expired ID document: Netbet suspends the account itself until a valid document is provided; no play, deposits or withdrawals. ADM may also set this state (reason 9). (Should be checked with the partner: Netbet cannot send reason 9, so the reason Netbet sends for its own suspension must be agreed.) After 60 days without a valid document the contract ends and the account is closed; the balance is paid on request. Guidelines 2.9.9.1; PACG 6.19, 6.11
  4. Closed account: no login (the sub-registration returns 1488). (Should be checked with the partner: the remaining balance and later credits are paid through customer support after checking the player's identity, and recorded on the closed account.) PACG 6.12
  5. Re-registration with the same credentials only 15 days after closure; it creates a new account with its own account code, ledger and opening message; the old account stays unchanged. Gaming account contract art. 15, 16; PACG 6.10 code 1209

E. Dormant accounts

  1. Block access to accounts with no movements for three years. Only stakes, wins, refunds, deposits and withdrawals count; logins, limit changes and bonus awards do not reset the clock. Guidelines 2.9.14, 2.9.14.1
  2. Identify them automatically, calculate the amounts due to the State (the balance without the bonus) and report each account to ADM immediately when the three years expire. ADM refuses the report if the account is already dormant (1400) or blocked (1401); 1515 refuses a legal-person account and 1217 a negative balance. Guidelines 2.9.14.1; PACG 6.14
  3. The dormant-account message 6.14 carries Data Dormiente (the date from which the account is dormant: three years after the last movement) and Importo Saldo (balance in euro cents net of any bonuses). The adapter is built with CHG-375322. PACG 6.14; Gaming account contract art. 18 c. 2
  4. Three years after the day of the last movement the account is considered extinct and the balance is due to the State. While bets on events not yet decided are open, the count waits; when they settle, the win or refund restarts it. Gaming account contract art. 18 c. 2
  5. On a dormant account the register refuses movements (1204 in 6.3), login or sub-registration (1489 in 6.12) and reopening (1210 in 6.10). PACG 6.3, 6.10, 6.12
  6. The register keeps two lists: 6.13 gives accounts not sub-registered for three years (states open, suspended, closed; migrated, dormant and blocked accounts excluded; 100 per request); 6.15 gives accounts dormant at a date with their balance net of bonuses. PACG 6.13, 6.15
  7. (Should be checked with the partner: the money is paid to the State by Netbet outside the platform. The platform provides the per-account list of amounts due and does not transfer money.)

6. Screens

  • Back office: suspend and block actions with the reason list, note field and the register of user and time.
  • Refusal pages at login, one per state (suspended, blocked, closed, dormant).
  • Profile: time-out (with the rules published on the site) and close account.
  • Dormant account: no gaming area and no cashier; a neutral message that the account is dormant after three years without movements, with a support contact.
