PSIP: fixed-odds horse-race betting

ADM's horse-race betting protocol (PSIP 3.0 of 3 September 2026): programme, bet sale, payment and refund, corrections, accounting and integrity, with the message catalogue and the current return codes.

Use with the task CHG-375325. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails. A rehearsal PSIP bench with fake data runs the cases of this kit in the browser.

At a glance

  • Fixed odds only. No pool (totalisator) bets and no own complementary programme.
  • Code before money. Every bet goes to ADM in real time. The stake is debited only after ADM returns the ticket code. A bet cannot be cancelled after the code.
  • Two-step payout. Payment or refund through PSIP (3011.7), then the credit through PACG (6.8). The balance moves only after both positive replies, within one hour of the official certification of the event.
  • Horse rules outside PSIP. Stakes in multiples of 3 euro, system limits, bookmaker margin, payout and dead-heat rules are applied by the platform (section C). PSIP has no field for them.
  • Daily duties. The accounting request (3500.1) compared with Digitain's figures, and the integrity message (3900.2) at least daily.
  • Source text. The kit follows the Italian original PSIP 3.0 of 3 September 2026. The English working translation is of the earlier text; where they differ, the Italian original of 3 September 2026 prevails.

Rules

A. Scope, transport and message exchange

  1. Scope: fixed-odds horse-race betting. Programme data, bet acceptance, results and payments go through PSIP. Pool (totalisator) bets and an own complementary programme are not part of the task. CHG-375325
  2. Every message has a header, a body and extended attributes (ea); the extended attributes follow the body. Maximum message size is 8 Kbytes. PSIP 4
  3. The header carries versione, fsc/cfs, id_cn, id_pvend, id_tag, tipo_tag, lung_body, lung_ae, id_transazione (8 bytes), id_servizio and id_messaggio. The header version and the provider fields are set at connection set-up with Sogei and are configurable: version 2 carries the connectivity provider, version 3 a service-provider concessionaire (CFS). PSIP 4.1
  4. Remote gaming uses virtual terminals (TIPO_TAG values in table 18.22). A TAG is a process in a pool that carries out one complete transaction with the totalisator; at most 50 TAGs. The client of the FSC/CFS services (programmes, accounting, signature) uses TIPO_TAG 0, at most 30. PSIP 4.1 note on TAG
  5. The client puts a transaction identifier in each request; the server returns it in the reply. A client without a reply repeats the request, keeping the identifier unchanged, after 2 seconds, then 4 seconds, then every 4 seconds. For every message that changes data the client keeps sending until it receives the reply. The server stores the reply of the last transaction of the client and returns it, without processing again, when the same identifier arrives. Two consecutive transactions must have different identifiers. PSIP 2
  6. Signed messages carry FIRMA_DIG01 (id 1093): MD5 hash, RSA 256-bit key, RSA_PKCS1_PADDING, 44 Base64 characters. Public keys are exchanged with 3800.1 (PEM format). PSIP 17.15, 15.1
  7. All components use UTC as time reference; reply timestamps carry TIME_STAMP_UTC. Link and message security follow CHG-375321. Guidelines 2.1.5; PSIP 17.52

B. Programme and betting window

  1. Only events of the ADM programme can be sold: programmes 3000.1, events 3000.2, outcome list 3000.3, official results 3000.7. Event state AP (acceptance open) is the only state in which a bet is taken; CH means acceptance closed, RU result official, AN cancelled. A sale on an event that is not playable returns 3001. PSIP 5, 18.4, 18.42
  2. Programme changes are sent by the totalisator in multicast (3001.1). Enabled providers check that they receive the notifications and retrieve missed ones on request: 3001.10 (last notification) and 3001.11 (a specific one; notifications of the last seven days; 2005 when the notification does not exist). PSIP 7, 7.2, 7.3
  3. Acceptance ends at the first start of the race, even if the start is not validated (for a race meeting, the first race). Bets accepted after that term give the right to a refund only. For ante-post races, acceptance ends at the official declaration of starters and no later than the day before the race. Det. 61681/RU art. 14 c. 3-5; DM 25/10/2004 art. 3

C. Horse betting rules that are not in PSIP

The platform applies these before sending a bet. They come from Italy's horse-racing betting rules and are checked by the testing lab. The task CHG-375325 holds the same rules with examples.

  1. Fixed odds: the unit stake is EUR 3.00 and larger amounts are multiples of EUR 3.00; no ticket below EUR 3.00 or not a multiple of EUR 3.00. Systems: the ticket is at least EUR 3.00, the unit stake is EUR 0.25, and the cost of a system is the sum of the amounts staked on its bets. DL 452/2001 art. 13 c. 1; Det. 61681/RU art. 10 c. 5-6; Guidelines 3.4.2, 3.4.3
  2. A multiple has at most 20 outcomes (also an ante-post multiple). A system has at most 30 races and may be of three kinds: full cover, correction (n outcomes taken k at a time, with optional fixed outcomes) and the two combined. Different bet types may be combined across the races of a system. Once the official runners are declared, the race can no longer be part of an ante-post multiple. Det. 61681/RU art. 9 c. 1, art. 10 c. 3-4; Guidelines 3.4.4, 3.4.14
  3. Official fixed-odds bet types are 20 Vincente (win), 21 Piazzato (place) and 23 Vincente no-betting, all single. PSIP 18.9; Det. 61681/RU art. 9 c. 2-4
  4. Odds are written with two decimals, are not below 1.00 and include the return of the stake. In PSIP the odds are in hundredths. Odds may change while acceptance is open if the change is made public. Bookmaker percentage limits for win bets: 2 to 3 starters 127%, 4 to 7 starters 139%, 8 to 9 starters 143%, 10 to 14 starters 147%, 15 or more 156%. Guidelines 3.4.5, 3.4.6; Det. 61681/RU art. 4 c. 5, art. 13 c. 1; DM 25/10/2004 art. 25 c. 5, art. 28
  5. Winnings. Single: odds times stake, truncated to two decimals. Multiple: product of the odds of the winning outcomes truncated to six decimals, times the stake, truncated to two decimals. System: sum of the amounts won by the bets that compose it. Always truncate, never round. Det. 61681/RU art. 13 c. 3, 5, 7; Guidelines 3.4.7, 3.4.8
  6. A winnings boost is optional and must be published in the acceptance programme; PSIP carries it in the MV_ attributes and the flag_mv field of each outcome. Det. 61681/RU art. 13 c. 8; PSIP 9.5, 17.29 to 17.35
  7. A refund is due, among other cases, when the race is cancelled, not validated or moved, when a bet includes a horse that did not start regularly, or when an outcome becomes unattainable. A bet with such a leg stays valid and the refunded leg gets odds 1.00; any winnings boost is recalculated without it. Ante-post bets are excluded from the non-starter and unattainable-outcome cases. Det. 61681/RU art. 15 c. 1-5
  8. Withdrawn horses on win and place bets: the odds are recalculated with the conversion table of annex 2 of the determination, once for each withdrawn horse, and the total reduction cannot exceed 90% of the agreed odds. The odds of all horses are sent to the totalisator with the sale so that the odds can be recalculated. Det. 61681/RU art. 16 c. 1, 4, 5
  9. Dead heat. Win, single bet: the odds stay, the payout is divided by the number of tied horses. Win, multiple: the odds are divided by the number of tied horses and the product is recalculated (six decimals), then multiplied by the stake and truncated to two decimals. Place: payout divided by the number of placed horses, times the number of places offered. Guidelines 3.4.9 to 3.4.11; DM 25/10/2004 art. 32, 33; Det. 61681/RU art. 17

D. Placing a bet

  1. Validate first, then move money. Every check that could reject the bet (limits, self-exclusion, risk, liability, balance, stake rule) runs before the bet is sent, and the stake is reserved before sending. The bet is accepted and the stake debited (cash and bonus parts) only after the central system returns esito 1024 with the ticket code. Any other reply: nothing is debited and the player is told. PSIP 9.5
  2. Sale messages: 3011.5 fixed-odds sale (single bets, also on complementary events; up to 20 entries, one ticket price and one maximum payout, each entry with its own odds); 3011.6 system sale (up to 30 base events; the request body at most 2000 bytes; at most 2000 multiples per ticket); 3011.1 is the earlier sale message without complementary events. PSIP 9.1, 9.5, 9.6
  3. Remote sale requests carry IP_RISP, ID_GIOCATA and FIRMA_DIG01 (mandatory), and CONTO_2, SALDO_CONTO and IP_VENDITA_GIOCATORE (mandatory for remote gaming); BONUS_PREZZO is optional; INFO_QUOTE is mandatory for bet types that allow non-starters on complementary events. The reply carries esito, id (the ticket ID, shown in hexadecimal), TIME_STAMP, TIME_STAMP_UTC, FIRMA_DIG01, CONTO_2 and TAG_MOV. (Should be checked with the partner: whether a direct connection sends the balance and player-IP attributes; the attribute chapters tie them to a service-provider concessionaire. This is in the task.) PSIP 9.5, 17.53 to 17.55
  4. SALDO_CONTO carries the account balance including bonuses and the bonus part, with bonus detail by game family and type. In the sale the balance is the balance after the price and the bonus played. BONUS_PREZZO is taken from the bonus balance in the order set by the attribute: the specific game type, then the family, then all games (family 0, type 0). PSIP 17.53, 17.54, 9.5
  5. ID_GIOCATA (id 1029) is year, day number in the year, and an ID unique within the day for each TAG. A repeated play identifier is refused with 3008 "Play identifier not unique". PSIP 17.16, 18.42
  6. No void after the code. A cancel message (3011.3) exists; for remote gaming ADM answers 4004 "ticket cannot be cancelled, remote gaming". For a remote ticket the receipt is the registration at the totalisator, accounted at once on the gaming account. Later changes go only through ADM: refund when the event is cancelled, result correction by reversal or correction messages. PSIP 18.42; Det. 61681/RU art. 1 w
  7. If the central system returns an error, the platform tells the player and stops the play. The reply 1517 means the sales service is temporarily closed by the totalisator. Guidelines 2.1.1.1; PSIP 9.5
  8. The gaming account state is checked at session start: after login a sub-registration is sent at once to the register and the session starts only after its reply. The register answers 1452 (cross-operator self-exclusion), 1487 suspended, 1488 closed, 1489 dormant, 1490 blocked, 1491 self-excluded from one or more game families. Guidelines 2.9.10.1; PACG 6.12

E. Settlement and the gaming account

  1. Settlement has two steps, both in real time. Step 1: send the win, refund or adjustment to the central system (3011.7) and store the positive reply with the amount. Step 2: send the credit (PACG 6.8) or reversal (PACG 6.9) to the gaming account register, with balance and bonus share. Credit the player account only after the PACG positive reply. Guidelines 2.1.2.1, 2.1.3.1; PSIP 9.7
  2. In 3011.7 the balance does not include the credit of the winnings or refund. The reply carries the amount in TAG_MOV: movement reason 3 (paid) or 4 (refunded) and the amount imp. Ticket information 3011.8 returns Importo, the actual amount to pay or refund. PSIP 9.7, 9.8, 17.49, 18.1
  3. PACG 6.8 credit: Id Ricevuta Pagamento is the ticket ID; movement reason 63 (payment), 65 (refund) or 67 (payment/refund); for horse-race fixed-odds betting PACG allows credit reasons 63, 65, 67 and reversal reasons 64, 66, 68; the bonus part of the credit is declared separately. PACG 6.8, 7.5, 7.8
  4. Reversal 3011.10 and 3011.12: the balance is not reduced by the amount to be reversed. Correction 3011.11 and 3011.13: the balance does not include the credit of the winnings; Importo is the actual amount to pay or refund. In PACG, 6.9 reverses the earlier credit (same receipt ID) and 6.8 then credits the amount now due. PSIP 9.10 to 9.13; PACG 6.8, 6.9
  5. There is no PACG message for a stake debit. PACG 6.3 and 6.4 cover top-ups, withdrawals and additional service costs. The register learns about the stake through the sale message, whose SALDO_CONTO is the balance after the price and bonus played. PACG 6.3, 6.4, 7.5; PSIP 17.54
  6. Winnings are credited within one hour of the official certification of the event that creates the win or refund. If the PACG credit is refused or times out, the account is not credited; the credit is retried with the same transaction ID until a definitive reply, and the one-hour limit still applies. Convention art. 11 c. 6 lett. a; Guidelines 2.9.17

F. Accounting and integrity

  1. The daily statement 3500.1 gives data by accrual date for a concessionaire and game type: levy rates and statement reasons with amounts. Reasons 16, 40 and 41 are returned with tipo_gioco 0; 40 and 41 are reported only for concessionaires of the new public games. Reason 11 is the single tax; aggregate and split tax lines are not summed blindly. PSIP 13.1, 18.2
  2. The daily ticket total 3500.3 gives the tickets issued and cancelled in a day. List of uncollected online tickets 3600.5: at most 250 tickets per request. Reconcile per game type and accounting day with ADM's replies; the figures trace back to individual tickets. What the platform displays is in CHG-375321. PSIP 13.3, 14.4
  3. Integrity 3900.2 is sent at least once a day and again immediately after corrective patches or bug fixes. The request lists the components (type 1 gaming platform, 3 gaming acceptance system, 4 gaming system). The digest is a message digest (SHA1 or MD5) of each critical binary file, listed in a text file; the unique code of a component is the digest of that text file. Non-compliant component data is code 7012 in the return-code table (the text of 3900.2 still shows the earlier value 8000); the component is blocked if the check fails. PSIP 16.2, 18.42; Guidelines 1.2, 1.3, 2.1.13
  4. Keep-alive 3900.1 tells the totalisator that the concessionaire is operational for gaming; it is sent once a minute. Services 3500, 3800 and 3900 are for the FSC/CFS client, not for the sale terminals. PSIP 16.1, 13.1, 15.1

G. Screens

  1. Bet detail and bet history show at least the ADM unique code, the amount debited, the gaming account code and the account holder's tax code. Account code and tax code may appear once per page (for example in a header or footer); each bet shows its ADM code and amount. The site and the apps are in Italian. Guidelines 2.3.2, 2.7.7
