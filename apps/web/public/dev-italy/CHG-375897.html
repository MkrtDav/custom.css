Account records: movements, retention, ADM access

The gaming-account ledger and register, how long data is kept and what ADM can query, the one-hour crediting rule and what the player sees about it, the reports ADM can ask for, and the monthly payout per casino game.

Use with the task CHG-375897. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails.

1. At a glance

  • Ledger. Every account operation is stored with its details; the balance is the sum of the items. Every top-up and withdrawal keeps the payment instrument and the payment provider's authorisation number. Kept for 10 years.
  • Register. An electronic register of gaming-account operations, searchable with simple reporting tools.
  • Corrections. ADM has no separate manual balance correction. A correction is a reversal of the original movement, linked to it.
  • Crediting within one hour. Wins and refunds are booked within one hour of the official certification of the event, of the player's request for sums from participation rights, or of the player leaving the game session or the tournament closing. Wins recognised after a complaint are included.
  • Crediting time visible. Bet history shows when each win or refund was credited; withdrawals show the request time.
  • ADM access. The last 6 months of stored information and 2 years of accounting information in real time, then at least 5 more years; ADM queries any time range; results within 48 hours, exportable.
  • Reports and payout. Reports for ADM, and the public monthly payout per casino game.
  • Bonuses are in CHG-375898; account states and dormant accounts are in CHG-376993.

What the platform builds

  • An append-only ledger: one entry per operation with time (UTC), type, cash and bonus parts, balance after, payment instrument and authorisation number; corrections are new entries linked to the movement they correct.
  • A crediting monitor: for each win or refund the trigger time, the booking time and the delay; a warning before the limit and an alert on a breach; a manual trigger, with reason and user, for wins recognised after a complaint.
  • The settlement chain: game protocol first, then the PACG credit (6.8 or 6.36); the account is credited only after the PACG positive reply; resend with the same transaction ID.
  • The account register with retention, deletion or anonymisation at the end of the period, and the query access for ADM.
  • Bet history, withdrawal and statement screens fed by the ledger.
  • Reports 1 to 3 and the public monthly payout page.

2. Rules

A. Ledger and statement

  1. For every gaming account the system stores at least, with the details of each operation: top-ups and reversals; withdrawals and reversals; debits of bets or stakes; credits of wins (tournament prizes included) and refunds; corrections, each linked to the movement it corrects; and the bonus items (see CHG-375898). All these items form the player balance. Guidelines 2.9.15, 2.9.15.1
  2. Details include the payment instruments used for top-ups and withdrawals. The system keeps a log of all authorisation numbers given by the payment service providers when approving a transaction; keeping it is a requirement, not an option. Only traceable payment instruments in the account holder's name are accepted. Guidelines 2.9.15.1, 2.9.20, 2.9.20.1, 2.9.3; Concession convention art. 15; Technical rules Part 2 ch. 1
  3. The balance shown to the player and the balance sent to ADM always match the ledger. ADM gives no database format, so the structure is ours.
  4. The player statement covers at least the previous 30 days, in time order. For each operation: timestamp, game type, reason, stake amount, wins or refunds, the unique ID of the participation receipt given by the central system, top-up and withdrawal information, bonus information. All components use UTC. Guidelines 2.9.19, 2.9.19.1, 2.1.5
  5. There is no PACG message for a stake debit; the stake travels in the game protocol. The register receives credits (6.8, 6.36) and reversals (6.9). PACG 6.3, 6.4, 7.5

B. Account register

  1. Keep an electronic register of gaming-account operations, searchable with simple reporting tools, holding: the player's identity data including the identity-verification results; account details and current balance; changes to account data including linked payment instruments; privacy consent; self-limits set and self-exclusions made since registration; details of any previous accounts of the player with the closure reasons; deposit and withdrawal history; betting history with at least game type, bet, amount staked and any win and/or refund. Previous accounts of the same player are linked by tax code. Technical rules Part 2 ch. 1

C. Retention and access for ADM

  1. Retention: itemised movements and plays are kept for ten years. Signed contracts are kept until the end of the tenth year after the concession expires. Personal data are kept for the contract and ten years after closing the account. The contract is the gaming-account contract the player accepts at registration and any later versions (different from the terms and conditions, which are part of it): store per player the version and the acceptance evidence (time, method, player ID). Gaming account contract annex 6 art. 5 c. 20, art. 12 c. 1 lett. c, art. 19 c. 4
  2. Apply in the system the retention periods stated in Netbet's privacy notices for self-exclusion and gaming-account data. At the end of the period delete or anonymise the data; the verification body checks this function.
  3. All stored information of the last 6 months and the accounting information of the last 2 years must be available in real time. After that, keep it for at least 5 more years with integrity, readability and access guaranteed. Guidelines 2.1.16, 2.1.16.1, 2.3.4.1
  4. ADM can run queries on that information for any time range it defines, for each component of the operator system. Results must be viewable within 48 hours at most and exportable. Guidelines 2.1.16.1; Technical rules Part 2 ch. 1
  5. Also available to ADM, in the way ADM defines: the personal data of each account holder, the balances and movements of the gaming accounts and the detailed data of each bet. Gaming account contract art. 12
  6. (Should be checked with the partner: the rules do not say how ADM runs these queries or receives the data; options are database access, the protocols, a back-office or BI tool, or Netbet running ADM's request and delivering the result within 48 hours.)

D. Crediting within one hour

  1. Wins and refunds are booked within one hour of the official certification of the event that creates them, or within one hour of the player requesting the sums available from participation rights, unless a game regulation says otherwise. This includes wins recognised after a player complaint. Concession convention art. 11 c. 6 lett. a; Gaming account contract art. 12 c. 3 lett. b; Guidelines 2.9.17
  2. Casino: within one hour of the player leaving the game session, or within one hour of the closing of the session for tournament card games. Guidelines 2.9.17.1
  3. The one-hour limit runs until the win or refund is booked on the gaming account. The PACG credit message (6.8) tells the central system that the credit has been made. The account is credited only after the PACG positive reply, so the whole chain, including the PACG reply, completes inside the hour. Concession convention art. 11 c. 6; PACG 6.8; CHG-375321
  4. If the PACG credit is refused or times out, the account is not credited; the credit is retried with the same transaction ID until a definitive reply, and the one-hour limit still applies. Netbet monitors the delay and alerts on breaches. CHG-375321
  5. Sums the player withdraws are credited to the player's payment instrument within seven days of the request. The operator answers complaints within 72 hours. Gaming account contract art. 12 c. 2, 3 lett. c; Concession convention art. 11 c. 6 lett. g

E. What the player sees

  1. The site and apps clearly show how and when winnings and refunds are credited and how and when withdrawals are made. The bet history shows when each win or refund was credited. Withdrawals: the balance is debited at the time of the request, which is already shown. The site and apps are in Italian. Guidelines 2.7.7

F. Reports for ADM

  1. Results are available within 48 hours at most. One list per report with all the listed fields; more detail can sit in the account view. Power BI or another reporting tool is allowed, but the verification body tests the reports end to end, so they must read complete platform data, history included. ADM's request is the usual trigger for producing a report.
  2. Report 1: all gaming accounts at a chosen date, with account status (open, closed, suspended, blocked, dormant for three years), the date of each status change, first name, surname, tax code, player ID and registration date. Technical rules Part 2 ch. 1
  3. Report 2: all gaming accounts, at a chosen date, where one or more deposits, withdrawals or wins exceeded a set threshold, per single transaction and in total over a period the user defines. Technical rules Part 2 ch. 1
  4. Report 3 (year-end balances): the balance of every gaming account (cash and bonus separately) at 31 December 23:59:59, exportable and reproducible later, for Netbet's yearly communication to ADM by 20 January. (Should be checked with the partner: ADM sets no protocol for it; Netbet confirms how it is sent.)

G. Monthly payout per casino game

  1. Show to anyone visiting the site and apps, with adequate visibility, the amount paid out in winnings relative to the turnover for each calendar month, at least for the month before publication. It applies to skill games, card games and fixed-odds chance games; remote bingo is out of scope for this project. Technical rules Part 2 ch. 7; Guidelines 2.7.5, 2.7.5.1
  2. (Should be checked with the partner: "adequate visibility" is not defined (placement, size); to be validated with the lab.)
  3. The rule does not prescribe a tool. Practical way: a public page (no login) with each game's actual payout percentage per past month, calculated automatically on the platform's own data and published by Netbet. (Should be checked with the partner: how bonus stakes count in the monthly figure, and the basis of the figure, since ADM's texts use different bases (amounts staked, turnover net of jackpots, amounts played). Make the numerator and denominator configurable until Netbet confirms the basis.)

6. Screens

  • Statement: a real table in time order: date and time, operation, cash part, bonus part, balance, reference. Filter by operation and date. Negative amounts carry a minus sign, not only colour.
  • Bet history: ADM code and amount on every bet; for a win or refund, the credit time, or "being credited" while the credit is in progress. Account code and tax code once per page.
  • Withdrawal detail: request time always visible; status; payment date when paid; the instrument masked.
  • Crediting explanation: text on site and app: wins and refunds are credited within one hour of the official result, of leaving the game session or of the tournament closing; the history shows each credit time; withdrawals show the request time.
  • Monthly payout page: public, no login, each game's payout percentage per past month.
