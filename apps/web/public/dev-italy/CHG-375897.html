Account records: movements, retention, ADM access

The gaming-account ledger and register, how long data is kept and what ADM can query, the one-hour crediting rule, the reports ADM can ask for, and the monthly payout per casino game.

Use with the task CHG-375897. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails.

At a glance

  • Ledger. Every account operation is stored with its details; the balance is the sum of the items. Every top-up and withdrawal keeps the payment instrument and the payment provider's authorisation number. Kept for 10 years.
  • Register. An electronic register of gaming-account operations, searchable with simple reporting tools.
  • Corrections. PACG 3.3 has no separate correction message; a correction is sent as a reversal of the original movement, linked to it, plus a new movement (should be checked with the partner and Sogei).
  • Crediting within one hour. Wins and refunds are booked within one hour of the official certification of the event, of the player's request for sums from participation rights, or of the player leaving the game session or the tournament closing. Wins recognised after a complaint are included.
  • Crediting time visible. Bet history shows when each win or refund was credited; withdrawals show the request time.
  • ADM access. The last 6 months of stored information and 2 years of accounting information in real time, then at least 5 more years; ADM queries any time range; results within 48 hours, exportable.
  • Reports and payout. Reports for ADM, and the public monthly payout per casino game.
  • Bonuses are in CHG-375898; account states and dormant accounts are in CHG-376993.

Rules

A. Ledger and statement

  1. For every gaming account the system stores at least, with the details of each operation: top-ups and reversals; withdrawals and reversals; debits of bets or stakes; credits of wins (tournament prizes included) and refunds; corrections, each linked to the movement it corrects; and the bonus items (see CHG-375898). All these items form the player balance. Guidelines 2.9.15, 2.9.15.1
  2. Details include the payment instruments used for top-ups and withdrawals. The system keeps a log of all authorisation numbers given by the payment service providers when approving a transaction; keeping it is a requirement, not an option. Only traceable payment instruments in the account holder's name are accepted. Guidelines 2.9.15.1, 2.9.20, 2.9.20.1, 2.9.3; Concession convention art. 15; Technical rules Part 2 ch. 9
  3. The player statement covers at least the previous 30 days, in time order. For each operation: timestamp, game type, reason, stake amount, wins or refunds, the unique ID of the participation receipt given by the central system, top-up and withdrawal information, bonus information. All components use UTC. Guidelines 2.9.19, 2.9.19.1, 2.1.5
  4. There is no PACG message for a stake debit; the stake travels in the game protocol. The register receives credits (6.8, 6.36) and reversals (6.9). PACG 6.3, 6.4, 7.5

B. Account register

  1. Keep an electronic register of gaming-account operations, searchable with simple reporting tools, holding: the player's identity data including the identity-verification results; account details and current balance; changes to account data including linked payment instruments; privacy consent; self-limits set and self-exclusions made since registration; details of any previous accounts of the player with the closure reasons; deposit and withdrawal history; betting history with at least game type, bet, amount staked and any win and/or refund. Previous accounts of the same player are linked by tax code. Technical rules Part 2 ch. 9

C. Retention and access for ADM

  1. Retention: itemised movements and plays are kept for ten years. Signed contracts are kept until the end of the tenth year after the concession expires. Personal data are kept for the contract and ten years after closing the account. Under the anti-money-laundering law, identity data, the date, value and payment instrument of each opening, top-up and withdrawal, and the IP address, date, time and duration of the connections used for them are kept for ten years from acquisition. The contract is the gaming-account contract the player accepts at registration and any later versions (different from the terms and conditions, which are part of it): store per player the version and the acceptance evidence (time, method, player ID), which is our design advice. Gaming account contract annex 6 art. 5 c. 20, art. 12 c. 1 lett. c, art. 19 c. 4; D.Lgs. 231/2007 art. 53 c. 3
  2. All stored information of the last 6 months and the accounting information of the last 2 years must be available in real time. After that, keep it for at least 5 more years with integrity, readability and access guaranteed. Guidelines 2.1.16, 2.1.16.1, 2.3.4.1
  3. ADM can run queries on that information for any time range it defines, for each component of the operator system. Results must be viewable within 48 hours at most and exportable. Guidelines 2.1.16.1; Technical rules Part 2 ch. 1
  4. Also available to ADM, in the way ADM defines: the personal data of each account holder, the balances and movements of the gaming accounts and the detailed data of each bet. Gaming account contract art. 12

D. Crediting within one hour

  1. Wins and refunds are booked within one hour of the official certification of the event that creates them, or within one hour of the player requesting the sums available from participation rights, unless a game regulation says otherwise. This includes wins recognised after a player complaint. Concession convention art. 11 c. 6 lett. a; Gaming account contract art. 12 c. 3 lett. b; Guidelines 2.9.17
  2. Casino: within one hour of the player leaving the game session, or within one hour of the closing of the session for tournament card games. Guidelines 2.9.17.1
  3. The one-hour limit runs until the win or refund is booked on the gaming account. The account is credited only after the PACG positive reply, so the whole chain, including the PACG reply, completes inside the hour. Concession convention art. 11 c. 6; Guidelines 2.1.3.1; PACG 6.8; CHG-375321
  4. If the PACG credit is refused or times out, the account is not credited; the credit is retried with the same transaction ID until a definitive reply, and the one-hour limit still applies. Netbet monitors the delay and alerts on breaches. CHG-375321
  5. Sums the player withdraws are credited to the player's payment instrument within seven days of the request. The operator answers complaints within 72 hours. Gaming account contract art. 12 c. 2, 3 lett. c; Concession convention art. 11 c. 6 lett. g

E. What the player sees

  1. The site and apps clearly show how and when winnings and refunds are credited and how and when withdrawals are made. Showing in the bet history when each win or refund was credited, and the request time of withdrawals, is our design; ADM requires a timestamp for each operation in the statement. The site and apps are in Italian. Guidelines 2.7.3, 2.7.7, 2.9.19; Technical rules Part 2 ch. 7

F. Reports for ADM

  1. Report 1: all gaming accounts at a chosen date, with account status (open, closed, suspended, or not moved for three years in ADM's list; blocked is a PACG state), the date of each status change, first name, surname, tax code, player ID and registration date. Technical rules Part 2 ch. 9
  2. Report 2: all gaming accounts, at a chosen date, where one or more deposits, withdrawals or wins exceeded a set threshold, per single transaction and in total over a period the user defines. Technical rules Part 2 ch. 9

G. Monthly payout per casino game

  1. Show to anyone visiting the site and apps, with adequate visibility, the monthly payout percentage (winnings paid relative to the sums collected, as in the RTP requirement) for each game application, at least for the month before publication. Whether the figure is net of jackpots should be checked with the partner and ADM. It applies to skill games, card games and fixed-odds chance games; remote bingo is out of scope for this project. Technical rules Part 2 ch. 7; Guidelines 2.7.5, 2.7.5.1; D.D. 666/2011 art. 9 c. 1 lett. c
