PSQF: fixed-odds sports betting

ADM's fixed-odds betting protocol (PSQF 5.2): event creation, bet sale, results and payment, result changes, odds errors, cash-out and accounting, with the message catalogue and return codes.

Use with the task CHG-375323. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails.

1. At a glance

  • Record first. Every bet goes to ADM in real time. The stake is debited only after ADM returns the ticket ID.
  • Two-step payout. Result, then payment to ADM (7900:2), then the credit to the gaming account (PACG 6.8). The balance moves only after both positive replies, and within one hour of the official certification of the result.
  • Offer. Only sports, competitions and bet types ADM has approved for Netbet. Each event is created with ADM before betting opens.
  • No cancel after acceptance. ADM refuses the cancel message for online bets. Cash-out is allowed, but not on bets placed even partly with bonus.
  • Corrections only through ADM. Wrong results and odds errors follow ADM's procedures. The platform never voids or re-prices a ticket on its own.
  • Stake, maximum win, system and payout rules are applied by the platform and are in the task CHG-375896. PSQF has no field for them.

What the platform builds

  • A PSQF gateway: header and body, signature, one request at a time per terminal (TAG), the reply stored by transaction ID, and the resend at 2 s, 4 s, then every 4 s.
  • A programme service: mapping of Digitain events and markets to ADM codes, registry and calendar reads, event creation and outcome lists, hiding what ADM has not confirmed.
  • A bet service: all checks, stake reservation, sale, debit only after the ticket ID, no cancel action anywhere.
  • A settlement service: results, payment, the PACG credit, the one-hour clock, reversals and corrections.
  • Cash-out, odds-error handling, the daily accounting requests with comparison against Digitain's own figures, keep-alive and integrity messages (CHG-375321).

Data to keep for each ticket: the ADM ticket ID, the transaction ID of every message, ID_GIOCATA, the price and its bonus part, every leg with its odds and INFO_QUOTA, each ADM reply with its TIME_STAMP_UTC, the PACG receipt, and the event and programme codes.

2. Rules

A. Official programme and offer

  1. Offer only the sports, competitions and bet types ADM has approved for Netbet. Map Digitain's events and markets to ADM's codes and hide for Italy anything that cannot be mapped. Guidelines 3.1.4, 3.1.4.1
  2. The official programme is made from the disciplines, competitions and bet types ADM has authorised. It is the only document on which bets are accepted. Guidelines 3.1.4, 3.1.4.1
  3. PSQF gives the registry codes (7100 queries) and the authorisation lists: 5.9 authorised bet models and 5.15 authorised competitions. The mapping from Digitain markets to these codes is built by Netbet and Digitain. PSQF 5.4 to 5.9, 5.15
  4. Create each event with ADM before betting on it opens (7200:1). Take it from ADM's calendar (read with 7100:20) or, if the calendar does not have it, create it as a new event under an approved sport and competition. PSQF 5.10, 7.1
  5. A new event carries sport, competition, date and time, the two teams (ADM team codes, or a description where a team is not in ADM's list) and AVV_FEED_PROVIDER, the feed provider that generated it (table 14.29; 0 = concessionaire). AVV_FEED_PROVIDER is mandatory when the event is not accepted from the calendar. PSQF 7.1 p. 73, 13.53, 14.29
  6. Reply to 7200:1: an event in ADM's calendar returns 1024 with the calendar code. An event already created by an operator returns 6022 "Event already created", and INFO_AVV (mandatory in that reply) carries the programme and event codes. PSQF 7.1 pp. 72-74, 13.21, 14.30
  7. Until ADM confirms the creation, the event is either not shown, or shown as an upcoming fixture without odds or betting option. Odds appear only once ADM has confirmed it.
  8. Markets and odds are not sent when the event is created. Bet types come from ADM's approved list. Only markets whose outcomes are built per event (outright winner, player lists) have their outcome list sent with 7200:3 before betting on them opens. Odds, and any line or handicap value, travel with each bet. PSQF 7.3, 8.1
  9. Keep the offer in line with registry and calendar changes. 7101:1 returns the last variation, 7101:2 a specific one. Variation attributes: INS_AVV_CAL (new calendar event), MOD_AVV_CAL (changed), ATT_DISATT_AVV_CAL (0 invalidates, 1 re-validates). PSQF 6.1, 6.2, 13.25 to 13.36
  10. Authorisation is also checked at sale: 3021 "Bet not authorised" and 3022 "Sport or competition not authorised". PSQF has no dedicated code for an unapproved competition at event creation; approval is read with 5.9 and 5.15. PSQF 14.30, 7.1

B. Bet placement

  1. Every bet is sent to ADM in real time. It is accepted, and the stake debited, only after ADM returns the ticket ID. All checks that could reject the bet (limits, self-exclusion, risk, balance) run before sending. Guidelines 2.1.1.1
  2. 7900:6 sells single or multiple bets: up to 30 bets, one price. 7900:7 sells a system: up to 30 base events. Duplicate events, bets or outcomes in one ticket are refused (3005, 3016, 3017). PSQF 8.1, 8.2, 14.30
  3. The ticket ID is 80 bits, written as 20 hexadecimal characters. It is the unique code the bet screens show. PSQF 14.8; Guidelines 2.3.2
  4. ID_GIOCATA identifies the play: year, day of year and an identifier unique within the day per TAG. 3009 "Play identifier not unique" refuses a repeat. ID_BIGLIETTO (13.47) is sent in a sale reply when the ID_GIOCATA already exists. PSQF 13.20, 13.47, 14.30
  5. PSQF is half-duplex: a client does not send while it waits for the reply to its previous request. A transaction is one request and one reply, identified by id_transazione. PSQF introduction, ch. 2
  6. No reply from ADM: resend the same request with the same transaction ID, after 2 s, then 4 s, then every 4 s, until a reply arrives. The bet is awaiting the reply meanwhile. The server returns the stored reply without reprocessing, for the last transaction of that client only. Two consecutive transactions must have different IDs. PSQF ch. 2
  7. The protocol sets no maximum wait: the client keeps sending until it has a reply for every message that changes data. Where betting cannot continue, the concessionaire informs the player and ADM and bears any full refund. PSQF 2; PSV 3; Tender technical rules
  8. For remote gaming a TAG is a process in a pool, at most 50 virtual terminals; 1540 refuses more. Each TAG carries one request at a time, so parallel bets need separate TAGs. PSQF 4.1 note, 14.30
  9. Messages are binary (fixed header, variable body, extended attributes), at most 4 KB each; the system-sale body is at most 2000 bytes. FIRMA_DIG01 is an MD5 digest signed with an RSA 256-bit key, PKCS1 padding, 44 base64 characters. IP_RISP gives the UDP port and IPv4 address for the reply. PSQF ch. 4, 4.3, 8.2, 13.19, 13.29
  10. Valid tipo_tag values for remote gaming: 5 Internet; 10-14 desktop browser; 20-24 desktop client; 30-34 mobile site; 40-44 smartphone app; 50-54 tablet app. A tipo_tag not enabled for the concessionaire returns 1522. PSQF 14.18, 8.1
  11. Odds have at most three decimals and are never below 1. INFO_QUOTA gives the representation: 1 hundredths, 2 thousandths. It is mandatory in requests 8.1 and 8.2, in the sale reply and in the 8.13 reply. Guidelines 3.1.5.1; PSQF 13.24, 8.13
  12. Live: a sale after the start time is accepted unless CHECK_INIZIO_AVVENIMENTO lists the events for which ADM must refuse once the start time has passed (3010). The sale reply flags events already in play in INFO_LIVE. PSQF 13.13, 13.22, 14.30
  13. Balance block: SALDO_CONTO in a sale is the account balance already reduced by the price and the bonus played. In payment, correction and cash-out messages it does not include the credit of the winnings. CONTO_2 carries the gaming account. PSQF 8.1 to 8.3, 13.15, 13.50
  14. Bonus on a bet: ADM has three bonus types: 1 full win; 2 reduced win (payment = win minus bonus used); 3 win in bonus (telematic bets only, bonus used equals the stake). Bonus used on one bet is at most EUR 100. A ticket sold with BONUS_PREZZO_2 carries BONUS_PAG_RIMB_2 in the payment and correction messages. Guidelines 3.1.9; DD 167944/RU art. 2-4; PSQF 14.21, 13.54, 13.55
  15. Winnings boosts use boost types 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 10 (percentage per event, fixed amount, per system, minimum number of events, tiered percentage), carried by MV_PERC_VAR, MV_IMPORTO, MV_IMPORTO_SISTEMA, MV_FASCE; the events need flag_mv. 3012 refuses an invalid boost. PSQF 13.37 to 13.43, 14.13, 14.30
  16. No cancellation after acceptance: ADM refuses the cancel message 7900:3 for online bets (4004). After a cash-out the refusal is 4005. PSQF 8.4, 14.30
  17. Header and connection: the header version and the provider and concessionaire fields come from Netbet's connection and Sogei at set-up and are configurable. Version 2 carries the identifier of the connectivity provider (FSC), version 3 that of a service-provider concessionaire. The concessionaire code (cn) is in the header for the ticket messages 8.1 to 8.12; other services use "FSC/CFS,0,0,TAG". Keep-alive and integrity messages are described in CHG-375321. PSQF 4.1, 4.2

C. Bet screens

  1. Bet detail and bet history show at least the ADM ticket ID, the amount debited for each bet, the gaming account code and the player's tax code. The account code and tax code may appear once per page (for example logged-in header or footer); a profile page alone is not enough. Guidelines 2.3.2; Tender technical rules Part 1 par. 1.1 point 11
  2. The gaming account statement lists the receipt ID of each bet. Guidelines 2.9
  3. The site and apps are in Italian (technical terms of common use excepted) and show how malfunctions are handled. ADM prescribes no wording for refusal messages. Guidelines 2.7.3, 2.7.7

D. Results and settlement

  1. Send official results to ADM with 7200:16 (per bet status: RU result, AN void with zero winning outcomes) or 7200:17 for bets with additional information. Refunds and cancellations follow the PSQF messages (7200:11 time-window refund, 7200:14 outcome status). PSQF 7.6, 7.7, 7.11, 7.14
  2. A positive reply to the result authorises the later payment or refund message. ADM does not settle on its own: the platform sends 7900:2 per ticket, with importo = the actual amount to pay or refund. The reply returns the amount paid. Guidelines 2.1.2.1; PSQF 8.3
  3. After the positive PSQF reply, credit the account through PACG 6.8. The balance moves only after both positive replies. Guidelines 2.1.3.1; PACG 6.8
  4. Wins and refunds are credited within one hour of the official certification of the event that determines the win or refund. The hour does not start when Digitain sends the result or when ADM replies. Tender technical rules Part 2 ch. 9; Guidelines 2.9.17, 2.9.17.1; Contract annex 6 art. 5, 12
  5. Win types: 1 win; 2 return as win; 3 half win and half return; 4 half return. A non-runner is outcome state N. Odds never fall below 1. PSQF 14.19, 14.4; Guidelines 3.1.7
  6. The payment return codes (5000 to 5022) contain no code for a wrong amount; 3008 and 3018 belong to the sale group. The calculation rules the platform follows (truncation for single and multiple bets, dead heat) are in CHG-375896. PSQF 8.3, 14.30; Guidelines 3.1.6 to 3.1.8

E. Result changes

  1. A wrong result is corrected through ADM: delete it with 7200:9, then send the correct result. PSQF 7.9
  2. A collected ticket that becomes losing is reversed with 7900:12 (amount to reverse). A collected ticket whose amount changes is corrected with 7900:13 (importo = amount to pay or refund). Each is followed by the PACG movement (6.9 reversal). PSQF 8.7, 8.8; PACG 6.9
  3. STORNO_NO_REF (13.44) allows reversing a collected ticket when the new result is not yet available, within one hour of the deletion of the wrong result. It cannot be used with MOD_LEGACY. Every ADM reply carries TIME_STAMP_UTC (13.46). 5008 is the generic refusal; 5017, 5018 and 5022 are specific. PSQF 13.44, 13.46, 8.7, 14.30

F. Odds errors

  1. Odds errors go only through ADM's procedure. The concessionaire files through an application provided by ADM, outside PSQF, within three days after the last date communicated to the totalizer for the event. Later requests are inadmissible (irricevibili). Only bet types with a static outcome list qualify. Determination 172904/RU art. 2 c. 1, art. 3 c. 1 and 3
  2. The request states company and tax code, concession codes, event, programme and event code, bet type and code, additional-information value, all outcomes, the outcome and odds claimed in error, the other outcomes' odds and the time interval. Documentary proof is needed only for live (post-start) collection. Determination 172904/RU art. 3 c. 2
  3. ADM recalculates the win of each ticket registered at the totalizer in the declared interval. 8000:8 lists the tickets of a recognised request; request states are 1 processing, 2 not recognised, 3 recognised, 4 improcedibile, 5 rejected. STORNO_ERRORE_QUOTA (13.45) reverses a collected ticket while a request is processed. Determination 172904/RU art. 6 c. 4, art. 8 c. 4; PSQF 9.8, 13.45, 14.22
  4. While a request is processed ADM answers 5021 to payment, 5020 to cash-out and 5022 to reversal and correction. The platform never voids or re-prices a ticket on its own. PSQF 14.30

G. Cash-out

  1. Full cash-out is 7900:18 (id, importo_cashout). Partial cash-out is 7900:19: id, prezzo_cashout, importo_cashout, and the price and win amount of the new remaining ticket. ID_GIOCATA, FIRMA_DIG01, CONTO_2 and SALDO_CONTO are mandatory for remote gaming. The reply returns the id of the new ticket and a sequence number. 7900:20 gives cash-out information. PSQF 8.10 to 8.12
  2. Cash-out is not allowed on bets placed even partly with bonus (5019). It is refused with 5020 while an odds-error request runs. DD 167944/RU art. 3 c. 3; PSQF 14.30

H. Accounting, reconciliation, integrity

  1. Request ADM's accounting figures with the 8000 messages and compare them with Digitain's own figures per accounting day. The daily statement is requested for a day given as year, month and day. What the platform displays is in CHG-375321. PSQF 9.1 to 9.9
  2. Every component of the concessionaire's system uses UTC as time reference. Guidelines 2.1.5
  3. 8004:1 keep-alive is sent every minute. 8004:2 software integrity check is sent at least daily and again after every patch. Components (14.26): 1 gaming platform, 3 game acceptance system, 4 game system; each is identified by id_componente, version and hash_componente. A failed automatic check blocks the component, and the concessionaire must then ask for a specific technical conformity check. PSQF 12.1, 12.2, 14.26; Guidelines 2.1.13

6. Screens

  • Bet slip: single, multiple and system; stake, odds, possible win and maximum win; stake steps of EUR 0.05; a confirming state while ADM has not replied (stake held, not spent); then the receipt with the ADM ticket ID.
  • Bet detail and history: the account code and the tax code in a header or footer on every bet page; for each bet the ADM ticket ID in full (20 characters), the amount debited (bonus part shown separately), event, market, selection, odds, possible win, and the state: being confirmed, accepted, won, lost, refunded.
  • Cash-out panel only where offered; not offered on bets placed even partly with bonus.
  • Refusal messages in Italian for stake step, maximum win, event closed, changed odds, insufficient balance, cancel not allowed and cash-out not available. ADM prescribes no wording.
