PSV: virtual-event betting

ADM's virtual betting protocol (PSV 2.0): schedules and events, bet sale, draw and official results, payment, recovery, accounting and operations, with the message catalogue and return codes.

Use with the task CHG-375324. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails. A rehearsal PSV bench with fake data runs the cases of this kit in the browser.

At a glance

  • Declare first. The programme and each event are opened at ADM before any bet. The draw request closes betting at ADM. Nothing is sold on an event ADM does not know.
  • Code before money. Check everything, reserve the stake, send the sale, and debit only after ADM returns the ticket code. No reply means no bet and no debit.
  • No void after the code. An accepted bet cannot be cancelled on the remote channel (4004). Only an event cancellation with refund changes it.
  • Two-step payout. Payment or refund through PSV (9100.2), then the credit through PACG (CHG-375322). The balance moves only after both positive replies, within one hour of the official result.
  • Bonus is shown as the part of the stake paid with bonus; bonus sums and winnings credited as bonus cannot be withdrawn (guideline 2.9.16), and the bonus is usable only for play.
  • Who sends the schedule, event, draw and result messages for Netbet is the FSC or CFS client named in the message definitions. This kit describes the messages and rules whoever sends them.

Rules

A. Declare first: programme and events

  1. A programme is the daily schedule of one discipline on one virtual platform. Its code is 2 digits of year, 3 digits of FSC/CFS and 5 digits of sequence, carried as 8 bytes (1299900001 travels as 000000004D7AE661). PSV 7
  2. A programme can be opened at most one day ahead of the request date. Only one programme per discipline can be coded for the current day (2007), and opening can be blocked on a date (2031). PSV 7.1
  3. Each event is declared to the central system before bets are accepted. The 9000.2 reply carries the event code and opens bet acceptance at the same moment. PSV 7, 7.2
  4. Only platforms known to ADM can be used: cod_piatt is the unique code ADM assigns to the virtual platform. Unknown platform 2008, blocked platform 2023, no enabled concessionaires 2009. PSV 7.1; Guidelines 5.3
  5. In an event opening, weights are in millionths and odds in hundredths; the event date and time are UTC. Wrong weights give 2004 and wrong declared odds 3004. PSV 7.2
  6. A multi-event is made of at most 10 simultaneous events of the same discipline. A tournament has rounds: the first round is sent with 9000.8, later rounds with 9000.9, and only when the events of the previous round have official results (2030). An event can be in one multi-event (2028) or one tournament (2029). PSV 7.7 to 7.9
  7. The presentation to the player shows the programme code, event code, description, participants, bet types, odds and a clock to the close of betting. An event is open at most eight hours; odds are shown at least 30 seconds before betting closes and winning outcomes at least 30 seconds after the event. Guidelines 3.3.1, 3.3.6, 3.3.10, 3.3.11

B. Code before money: bet acceptance

  1. The sale is sent to the central system in real time. The account is moved only after the ticket code has been received and stored; if the central system answers with an error, the player is told and play does not start or continue. Guidelines 2.1.1.1
  2. No reply: the bet is not accepted and nothing is debited. The client keeps sending the same request with the same transaction ID until it receives the reply. PSV 3
  3. The sale (9100.1) carries the price in cents, the maximum payout and up to 20 bet lines; each line has the programme, the code of the event, multi-event or tournament, the bet type, the odds in hundredths and the outcomes in order. PSV 8.1
  4. Mandatory extended attributes on a sale: IP_RISP, ID_GIOCATA and FIRMA_DIG01; for remote gaming also CONTO_2, SALDO_CONTO and IP_VENDITA_GIOCATORE. The balance is sent already reduced by the ticket price and by any bonus played. PSV 8.1
  5. ID_GIOCATA identifies the play and is unique within the day for each TAG; a repeat with a new transaction ID gives 3008. PSV 13.2, 14.16
  6. The 1024 reply carries the ticket ID: a 48-bit field (bit[48]) shown in hexadecimal (12 hex digits), the top 8 bits EC for the gaming circuit and 40 bits assigned by ADM. PSV 14.5
  7. Bet detail and bet history show at least the ADM code, the amount debited, the gaming account code and the account holder's tax code; the ADM code is the only bet code shown. Guidelines 2.3.2.1; Technical rules Part 1 par. 1.1 point 11
  8. Remote bets cannot be cancelled: 9100.4 returns 4004 for remote gaming. Later changes go only through ADM: a refund when the event is cancelled, and the odds-error procedure. PSV 14.16
  9. Bonus: PSV has one bonus type (1). BONUS_PREZZO states the bonus part of the price and the bonus balances (game family and type) it is taken from; SALDO_CONTO states the balance including bonus, the bonus part and the bonus balance detail. The worked example is in section 4. PSV 13.11, 13.12, 14.13 to 14.15
  10. Winnings boost MV_PERC_VAR: with five events at odds 2.00 (product 32.00) and a 10 per cent boost, min_avv 5 applies the boost once (35.20) and min_avv 3 applies it three times (42.59); with one event cancelled the first case gets no boost and the second two applications. PSV 13.6
  11. A multiple cannot mix events with multi-events or tournaments (3015). Duplicate events give 3005, duplicate bets 3016, duplicate systems 3017. PSV 14.16
  12. A system bet sale (9100.5) has at most 10 base events and at most 512 multiples; the system code is the number of events in each multiple (1 single to 10 ten-fold). PSV 8.5, 14.10; Guidelines 3.3.4

C. Stake, win and presentation limits for virtual betting

These limits are in ADM's guidelines (quoting D.D. 3759/2016 and D.M. 145/2022), not in the PSV text. The platform applies them before sending; PSV also has the return codes 3006, 3007, 3013, 3018 and 3019 for a wrong ticket price, a wrong payout amount, the return limit with a boost, incorrect pricing and a maximum platform odds breach; the PSV text does not say which limit triggers which code.

  1. Unit stake 0.50 euro; systems 0.05 euro. Minimum 1 euro per ticket, in multiples of 50 cents (systems: multiples of 5 cents). Guidelines 3.3.2, 3.3.2.1; D.D. 3759/2016 art. 12
  2. Maximum potential win 50,000 euro per ticket. For systems the total win is the sum of the winning bets and cannot exceed the cap of D.D. 47010/2009 art. 4; a system made of single or multiple bets with a potential win over 10,000 euro cannot be sold. Guidelines 3.3.3, 3.3.3.1
  3. Systems: integral, correction and integral correction systems; at most 10 events, 100 outcomes (including opposing ones) and 512 combinations; events of different disciplines cannot be combined. Guidelines 3.3.4; PSV 8.5
  4. RTP set on the platform: 80 to 90 per cent for single bets and 60 to 90 per cent for bets on several outcomes of one event; for a multiple bet (outcomes of several events) the theoretical return is at most 90 per cent, calculated after any winnings boost and also in case of refund. Guidelines 3.3.5
  5. Message limits: at most 20 bet lines per sale; at most 10 events in a multi-event; system sale body at most 1500 bytes. PSV 8.1, 7.7, 8.5

D. Draw, official results, closing

  1. The draw request (9000.3) closes bet acceptance at the same moment and returns the pseudo-random numbers. ADM guarantees that acceptance is closed before the random combinations are generated, and the request must not come more than eight hours after the event was opened. PSV 7.3; Guidelines 3.3.11.1
  2. After the event the platform sends the official results (9000.4) for the event, multi-event or tournament. If they differ from those calculated by the totalisator (2024), payments on that event are suspended and payment requests answer 5005. PSV 7.4, 8.2, 14.16
  3. An event, multi-event or tournament is in state 2 (open), 3 (closed), 4 (result entered) or 7 (cancelled). PSV 14.2

E. Settlement in two steps

  1. Step 1, PSV. 9100.2 pays and/or refunds a ticket: the request carries the ticket ID and the actual amount; the reply gives the ticket state and the amount paid. The account balance sent with it excludes the credit and any bonus won. Refusals: 5003 official results missing, 5001 not payable, 5002 already paid, 5004 time-barred, 5005 suspended, 5006 different concessionaire, 5007 cancelled. PSV 8.2, 14.16
  2. Step 2, PACG. After the PSV positive reply, send the credit to the gaming account register (6.36 or 6.8) with the balance and the bonus share; credit the player's account only after the PACG positive reply. For fixed-odds virtual betting (game family 10, type 1) the PACG table lists the credit reasons 63 payment, 65 refund and 67 payment/refund. PACG 7.5, 7.8
  3. If the PACG credit is refused or times out, the account is not credited; the credit is retried with the same transaction ID until a definitive reply. PACG answers 1027 for a request already processed. PACG waits 4 seconds for the first three retries, 10 seconds for the fourth and fifth, 30 seconds from the sixth; the betting protocols use 2, 4, 4 seconds. PACG 2.1; PSV 3
  4. Winnings and refunds are credited within one hour of the official certification of the event that determines them. Guidelines 2.9.17
  5. Time-bar: a time-barred ticket takes states 16, 17 or 18 and payment answers 5004; 9200.2 lists time-barred tickets. Online winnings are credited to the gaming account automatically. PSV 14.3, 9.2; ADM page on collecting virtual-betting winnings

F. Malfunctions and recovery

  1. All virtual events open at the time of a malfunction must be cancelled by the acceptance system (9000.5). The technical cancellation refunds every bet on the event in full. Guidelines 4.1.1; PSV 7.5
  2. Multiples that include a cancelled event stay valid, but the odds of the cancelled leg do not count towards the potential win of the ticket. A multi-event or tournament containing a cancelled event is cancelled automatically with all bets refunded, and no event can be added to such a tournament. Guidelines 4.1.1
  3. An event in the closed state cannot be cancelled. The random numbers were already sent, so the event is completed once the malfunction is solved, even without showing the run, and the official results are always sent. Guidelines 4.1.1
  4. On restart the platform resumes the scheduled events and drops those it did not handle. PSV recovers lost information: 9400.1 lists events, multi-events and tournaments left open or closed for an earlier date (the totalisator must not yet have calculated the accounting data for that date, at most 100 elements); 9400.2 returns the random numbers already sent for a closed event dated before the request. Guidelines 4.1.1; PSV 10.1, 10.2

G. Accounting and reconciliation

  1. The daily statement (9200.1) gives, per accrual date, the number and amount for each accounting reason (table in section 4). The platform reconciles per game type and accounting day with this reply (platform advice: run it on the platform's own transaction data). What the platform displays is in CHG-375321. PSV 9.1, 14.4
  2. Ticket lists: time-barred tickets at most 20 per request (9200.2), uncollected online tickets at most 180 (9200.3); daily totals of tickets issued (9200.4) and issued and cancelled (9200.5). PSV 9.2 to 9.5
  3. All components use UTC as the time reference; logs and message timestamps refer to a UTC source. Guidelines 2.1.5

H. Operations, security and transport

  1. Keep-alive (9500.1) is sent every minute. The integrity message (9500.2) is sent at least daily and again immediately after corrective patches or bug fixes; it lists component type, ID, version and hash, and 7012 means the component data are not compliant. Both are described in CHG-375321. PSV 12.1, 12.2
  2. Integrity checks are automated. A failed or impossible check is stored with date and time and the component may keep running temporarily; if the check fails again after 15 calendar days the component is blocked. Guidelines 2.1.13, 2.1.13.1
  3. FIRMA_DIG01 signs the whole message (header, body and extended attributes) except the signature itself: MD5 digest, RSA key of 256 bits, RSA_PKCS1_PADDING, 44 characters in base 64. Public keys are exchanged in PEM format with 9300.1. PSV 6.2, 13.4, 11.1
  4. The protocol is half-duplex: a client cannot send while it waits for a reply. The client sets a transaction ID (8 bytes) different for two consecutive transactions; the server stores the reply of the last transaction and returns it unchanged for a repeated ID. For messages that change data the client keeps sending until it has the reply. PSV 1, 3
  5. A message is at most 4 Kbytes; numeric fields are big endian; strings are ISO 8859-1; the response destination is given in IP_RISP (IP address and UDP port). PSV 4, 5, 13.3
  6. Remote gaming uses virtual TAGs (a pool of processes, at most 50 for types 3 to 54; at most 30 for TIPO_TAG 0 service clients). A sale with a TAG type not enabled for the concessionaire answers 1522. PSV 6, 14.11, 14.16
  7. One modular and independent interface per protocol and per game platform; audit logs of operator and administrator actions on every component (guideline 2.1.11). Guidelines 2.5.1, 2.1.11, 2.1.11.1
  8. MOD_LEGACY (attribute 2503) lets a concessionaire carried by a CFS pay, reverse and correct an online fixed-odds ticket that was sold by a concessionaire on behalf of an FSC, when that concessionaire and the gaming account used for the sale were later acquired by the concessionaire carried by the CFS. Otherwise payment goes to the concessionaire that sold the ticket and any other concessionaire gets 5006. PSV 13.14, 14.16
  9. Legal-person accounts exist in the register protocol only for three technical uses (special shares of pools, play in the production environment, technical-functional checks), so 3022 (price not valid for a legal-person account in the production environment) concerns those accounts; that it does not affect ordinary players is our reading. PACG 7.16, 7.22; PSV 14.16
  1. Bet acceptance runs from 07:00 to 03:00 and at most 3,000 virtual events a day are in the official programme, unless ADM changes them; the events of a programme can be opened only within the same day. (Check the current ADM provision: the source text is of 2016.) D.D. 3759/2016 art. 6; PSV 7.1
  2. Winning outcomes are available to the player for at least 3 days and at any time on request. D.D. 3759/2016 art. 19 b
  3. Winnings boosts are only for multiple bets, only from systems authorised by ADM, and must be made known to players in the programme; the boost information is sent with the sale. The odds stay unchanged during the whole acceptance period of an event and are not below or equal to 1.00. D.D. 3759/2016 art. 13 c. 3-4, art. 14
  4. The header field versione is 2 when the client carries an FSC id and 3 when it carries a CFS id. PSV 6

Bet placement

  1. Checks
    Limits, self-exclusion, suspension, risk, balance, stake rules. If one fails the bet is refused and nothing is sent.
  2. Reserve
    The stake is reserved.
  3. Send
    9100.1 with transaction ID T, a unique ID_GIOCATA for the day, signed.
  4. 1024
    Debit cash and bonus, store the ticket ID, show the ADM code.
  5. Refusal code
    Release the stake, tell the player, no debit.
  6. No reply
    Resend the same transaction ID after 2 s, 4 s, then every 4 s. Nothing is debited.
