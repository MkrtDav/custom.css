PACG: the gaming account register

ADM's gaming account register protocol (PACG 3.3): account opening, login check, money movements, bonus, credits, states, self-limits and self-exclusion, with the message catalogue and return codes.

Use with the task CHG-375322. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails.

1. At a glance

  • Record first. Deposits, withdrawals, reversals, bonus awards and bonus reversals are sent to ADM before the account moves. Each message carries the balance after the operation with its bonus share.
  • ADM state rules. Login, deposits and withdrawals are refused by account state and reason. Netbet never reopens an account that ADM suspended or blocked.
  • Registration. Two routes: SPID/CIE, or document plus KYC provider. The account is activated only after ADM's OK, the player's confirmation of the account code, the credentials and the three daily limits.
  • Self-limits and self-exclusion. Four limit types by four calendar periods; four exclusion variants, fixed (7 to 270 days) or indefinite, with revocation from day 270 taking effect after 7 days.
  • Closure. A closed account is never reopened; re-registration creates a new account after 15 days.
  • Bets and wins are not PACG messages. They go through the game protocol of each game type; PACG receives only the credit of the win and the declared balance.

What the platform builds

One PACG adapter on top of the shared mechanics in the common-rules kit (CHG-375321): the message catalogue in section 4, the balance block on every movement, the return-code mapping to player messages, the state and reason rules of 6.10, the self-limit and self-exclusion timelines, and the platform-side rules the protocol does not enforce (7, 15 and 180 days). PACG messages are SOAP over HTTPS with WS-Security (signed username token, signed body, encryption for some requests). On a lost reply the same request is resent unchanged: 4 s three times, 10 s twice, then every 30 s.

2. Rules

A. Registration and account opening

  1. Before 6.1 is sent the platform has identified the player and verified the identity: identity data and a valid ID document (entered, fetched from a KYC provider, or taken from SPID/CIE), tax code, the gaming account contract accepted (different from the T&C), and the three daily self-limits set. A positive ADM reply does not replace Netbet's own identity check. PACG 6.1
  2. 6.1 is sent only after the documents are acquired. Sent as "not acquired" it is refused (1449). Until it is sent and answered, the registration is not a gaming account: no deposit, no play. PACG 6.1, table 7.20
  3. First-activation caps for the three daily self-limits: connection time 3 hours, spend (staked minus won and refunded) 100 EUR, deposits 200 EUR; for players aged 18 to 24: 2 hours, 50 EUR, 50 EUR. Lower values are accepted; a higher value is refused (1454). The lower 18 to 24 caps apply at first activation only and the age is read at the opening request. ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.9, 2.9.9.1; Concession agreement art. 20 c. 3 (g, h); ADM FAQ 12 March 2025; PACG 6.1 code 1454
  4. After activation the player changes the limits under the normal rules (rule 33). The 25th birthday has no effect on an account that is already active. ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.9.1; PACG 6.23
  5. Activation happens only after the positive reply (1024), the player's confirmation that the unique account code was received, and the login credentials and self-limits being set. ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.9, 2.9.9.1
  6. Account code confirmation: the player submits, the platform generates the unique account code and sends it in 6.1; after ADM accepts, the final screen shows the code with an unchecked box "Confermo di aver ricevuto il codice identificativo del mio conto di gioco: {codice_conto}"; the platform records the code shown, the exact wording and the timestamp. ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.9
  7. Two separate consents, each recorded with wording, version and timestamp: (1) the gaming account contract before submission, with the separate approval of the clauses under Civil Code art. 1341 and 1342 and the consent to send the data to ADM; (2) the code receipt after ADM's OK.
  8. If the player leaves without confirming the code, the account stays open at ADM and no 6.10 status change is sent, because PACG has only the states open, suspended, closed, dormant and blocked. In the platform the account waits in a code-confirmation state with deposits and betting blocked; on return the same code is shown. PACG table 7.2
  9. Route A, SPID or CIE at level 2 or higher: the data come from the digital identity; the player adds nickname, password, the three limits and the contract acceptance. No document upload, but Netbet must be able to obtain the ID and tax-code copies from the identity provider and supply them within 10 days of an authority's request. Personal-data type sent: 2 (SPID) or 3 (CIE). PACG table 7.15
  10. Route B, ID document plus KYC provider: the player enters the contact, nickname, tax code, residence, password, limits and contract acceptance; the provider's approved result supplies the document data. The tax code is checked against name, sex and birth data, and a mismatch is fixed before sending. The document copies are kept. Personal-data type sent: 1 (manual). PACG table 7.15
  11. 6.1 carries: unique account code, tax code, surname, name, sex, birth date, municipality and province, residence, document type, number, issue date, issuing authority, place of issue and expiry date, e-mail, nickname, personal-data type, documents acquired, and the three daily limits. Not sent: mobile number, password, document images. PACG 6.1
  12. Refusals of 6.1 leave the account not opened, with the reason shown to the player: 1200 already holds an account with Netbet, 1302 minor, 1303 invalid personal data, 1304 invalid tax code, 1449 documents not acquired, 1451 document expired, 1452 cross-operator self-exclusion in progress, 1454 self-limit value not allowed at opening. PACG 6.1
  13. Identity data (and for route B the document copies), top-up and withdrawal records and the connection data (IP, date, time, duration) of those operations are kept for 10 years; for route A also the digital-identity expiry date. Gaming account contract art. 5 c. 20, art. 19 c. 4
  14. After opening, if the ID document expires or becomes invalid, or the data are incomplete or incorrect, the account is suspended: no play, deposits or withdrawals until the player fixes it. After 60 days without a fix the contract ends and the account is closed (6.10), the balance is paid on request, and the player returns only by a new registration. ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.9.1

B. Login check

  1. At every session start the platform sends the sub-registration request 6.12, which is mandatory before the session starts. The session is refused for 1487 suspended, 1490 blocked, 1488 closed, 1489 dormant, 1452 cross-operator self-exclusion, 1491 self-exclusion from game families. During a session a refused operation (1271, 1471) ends the session. PACG 6.12
  2. Account states are picked up at login, not polled. When ADM lifts a state, the next login check returns OK. Netbet never reopens an account that ADM suspended or blocked; accounts Netbet suspended are reopened with 6.10 (new state open).
  3. Two-factor login, the previous-login display and unauthorised-access alerts are in the task CHG-375899; this kit covers the PACG check.

C. Deposit, reversal and top-up points of sale

  1. A storage request goes to the central register before every deposit, withdrawal, reversal of either, bonus award and bonus reversal (6.3, 6.4, 6.5, 6.6). Each carries the balance after the operation with the bonus share. The account is moved only after the positive reply. PACG 6.3 balance block; ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.8.1
  2. Only traceable payment instruments in the holder's own name are accepted (PVR cash is the only exception, rule 23). Each deposit carries the PACG payment-method code (table 7.9) and every platform or PSP method maps to one code; a code not allowed for the movement is refused (1421). PACG table 7.9, code 1421
  3. The authorisation number returned by the payment provider is logged for every deposit. Deposits are free for the player; additional service costs may be declared only on withdrawals.
  4. Reversal 6.4: a normal reversal within 15 days, a charge-back reversal within 196 days (28 weeks); later ones are refused (1239). PACG 6.4, code 1239
  5. A deposit is refused when it would exceed the deposit self-limit (1494), during self-exclusion, on blocked (1271), closed (1203) and dormant (1204) accounts, on accounts suspended for reasons 9, 10, 11 (1471), and while the account is waiting for the code confirmation. The deposit limit is checked as the sum of the top-ups in the current period of the limit. PACG 6.3; ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.1.1
  6. Top-up points of sale (PVR): only PVRs on ADM's register with a contract with Netbet; a PVR that loses either is blocked at once. A PVR may top up, open and close accounts after identifying the holder, never pay withdrawals or winnings.
  7. Cash or untraced instruments at PVRs: at most 100 EUR per player per week across all PVRs (Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59), checked before sending; an excess top-up is refused (1495). Traceable instruments already validated by Netbet have no PVR cap. 6.3 carries ID PVR above zero only for PVR top-ups. Codes: 1111 PVR not identified, 1112 not Netbet's, 1113 not enabled, 1496 method allowed only for PVR. PACG 6.3, table 7.11

E. Withdrawal

  1. A withdrawal is recorded first like a deposit and goes only to a payment account or instrument in the holder's own name among bank transfer, direct debit, payment card, prepaid card and other services of an Italian or EU payment provider that identify payer and payee. The concessionaire checks that the instrument belongs to the holder and refuses otherwise. A withdrawal is never paid at a PVR or other point of sale, so method 17 is refused on a withdrawal. ADM determination 675265/RU of 22 October 2026 art. 2(1), 5(1) to 5(4); PACG 6.3 table 7.9

D, F, G. Play credits, bonus, statement

  1. Bets and wins are not sent with PACG: they go through the game protocol of each game type (PSQF, PSV, PSIP, PGDA). PACG has no message for the stake debit; the register sees it through the balance declared in the next PACG message.
  2. Wins, refunds and cancellations are credited with 6.8 or 6.36 quoting the game ticket or receipt ID; reversals with 6.9. The account is credited only after the positive reply; a refused or unanswered credit is retried with the same transaction ID until a definitive reply. Credit types: sports, virtual and horse fixed-odds betting accept payment, refund, payment/refund; casino and cash card games accept payment only. PACG 6.8, 6.9, 6.36
  3. The return-code lists of 6.8, 6.9 and 6.36 contain no refusal by account state, so late wins and refunds on a closed, blocked or suspended account are accepted by ADM. The state limits only what the player can then do. PACG 6.8, 6.9, 6.36 compared with 6.3; ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.17
  4. Bonus award (6.5) and bonus reversal (6.6, cancellation or conversion into cash) are recorded first like a deposit. Bets placed with bonus are not sent with these messages; the bonus share goes in the game-protocol message and the declared balance. Reversal window: 365 days from the award (1427 after that). PACG 6.5, 6.6
  5. A player self-excluded across operators, and per product for the excluded product, receives no bonus. 6.5 refuses a bonus award to an excluded player with 1298 "the person is self-excluded". A bonus reversal on a closed account is not refused (6.6 has no 1203). ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.4, 2.9.4.1; PACG 6.5 code 1298, 6.6
  6. One itemised statement, in time order, of every movement that makes up the balance: deposits, withdrawals, stakes, wins, refunds, bonus awarded, cancelled and converted, and the bonus part of each stake and win. Platform operation types map to the PACG reason codes.

H, I. Self-limits and operator-imposed limits

  1. Self-limits: four types (time, spend, money lost, deposits) by four periods (day, week, month, year). Time is connection time from login to logout on any device; spend is staked minus won and refunded; money lost is deposits minus withdrawals. Periods are calendar periods: day 00:00:00 to 23:59:59, week Monday to Sunday, calendar month, calendar year. No unlimited value; on site and app. ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.1.1; PACG table 7.17
  2. Changes (6.23): a stricter value applies at once; a higher value from the seventh day; raising a first-activation limit from the next day; a change of the period from the seventh day. Activity already in the current period counts toward the new value. 6.23 returns 1460 if period and value equal the previous ones. PACG 6.23 codes; ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.1, 2.9.1.1
  3. Sent to the central system: the money-lost limit whenever set, every change (6.23) and every breach (6.25: category, value reached including the excess, date and time). PACG 6.23, 6.25
  4. At a breach of any self-limit all gaming stops at once in all active sessions, not only the limited activity; started activities complete; login stays for account management and limit changes. A deposit that would exceed the deposit limit is refused, so the limit is reached and not exceeded, and play with the existing balance continues.
  5. The block ends only when a new period starts or a raised limit takes effect; lowering keeps the block and customer support cannot lift it.
  6. Operator-imposed limits are separate from self-limits (the stricter applies) and the player is told at least 7 days ahead, also for individual risk measures. The ADM guidelines add that they are communicated to the central system through the protocol. Self-limit categories in 6.23 are the four of table 7.18 (top-up, spend, connection time, loss); another category is refused with 1502. ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.1.1; PACG 6.23, table 7.18

J. Self-exclusion

  1. Four variants, each fixed-period or indefinite: all operators with all games, Netbet only with all games, all operators with selected categories, Netbet only with selected categories. 6.16 types 5 to 12 (table 7.13). Category exclusion is mandatory: an "all games" option alone fails the verification. Only the categories Netbet offers are shown (sports betting, virtual betting, horse betting, casino), mapped to the PACG game families (table 7.8). PACG 6.16, tables 7.8, 7.13
  2. Fixed period: 7 to 270 days in the duration field (Durata); the duration is 0 for an indefinite exclusion and for a reactivation. Presets such as 7, 30, 60, 90, 180 and 270 days are valid; a free number is limited to 7 to 270; the end date is shown before confirming. Types 1 to 4 belong to the older message 4.24. PACG 6.16, table 7.13
  3. At self-exclusion the platform sends 6.16 at once and disconnects all active sessions; for a category exclusion only the sessions in the excluded categories close. Started activities complete (open bets settle and winnings can be withdrawn; hands, tournaments and rounds already started finish), decided by game type; anything new is blocked. ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.4.1
  4. Blocked: play, deposits and bonuses (only the excluded categories for a category exclusion); a cross-operator exclusion also blocks opening accounts with any operator. Kept: login to manage the account (withdraw, view statements, change limits, extend to indefinite, request revocation when allowed). A single-operator exclusion blocks, for that operator, play, top-ups and bonuses; withdrawals are not listed as blocked. PACG table 7.13; ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.4, 2.9.4.1
  5. Before opening an account the platform checks the tax code against the self-exclusions in force that day, at Netbet and across operators, and refuses opening for types 5 to 8 (the central system refuses it too, 1452). A category-only exclusion (types 9 to 12) does not block opening. A cross-operator self-exclusion may be registered only for people who have or had an account with Netbet (1293). PACG 6.1, 6.16
  6. A fixed exclusion can become indefinite on the player's request (6.16); an indefinite one cannot become fixed. ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.4.1
  7. Revocation of an indefinite exclusion is self-service in the logged-in profile, available from day 270 after the self-exclusion, with a confirmation step and responsible-gaming information; it is recorded and sent with 6.16 (management = reactivation, type 1, duration 0). Reactivation is automatic with no operator approval. Refusals: 1484 under 270 days, 1432 registered through another operator. PACG 6.16 codes 1484, 1432, table 7.13; ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.4.1
  8. Self-limits set before the self-exclusion stay in force after revocation and on every return to play, also when a fixed period expires; a raise already requested keeps its own date.
  9. Every self-exclusion, change and revocation made at Netbet is reported with 6.16. Exclusions registered elsewhere are learned through 6.17 (one person, only for holders of an active account with the operator, else 1433), 6.18 (open, suspended and blocked accounts of excluded persons, 100 per call) and 6.35 (accounts of persons excluded since the previous call, at most once a minute, last 200). PACG 6.17, 6.18, 6.35; ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.4.1

K. Account states, suspension and block

  1. States: 1 open, 2 suspended, 3 closed, 4 dormant, 5 blocked. Reasons Netbet sends with 6.10: 2 requested by the concessionaire, 3 by the holder, 6 documents not sent, 7 suspected fraud, collusion or use by third parties, 10 on behalf of the judicial authority. Reasons 1, 5, 9 and 11 are set by ADM. Reason 8 (self-exclusion) has no message enabled; no status change is sent for a self-exclusion. Reason 4 is for the older message 4.5 only. PACG tables 7.2, 7.3, 6.10
  2. 6.10 refusals: 1208 to 1210 reopen from open, closed, dormant; 1211 to 1213 and 1507 suspend; 1214, 1215 close; 1275, 1276, 1505, 1506 block (a block follows a suspension); 1234 invalid reason; 1514 legal-entity account. The platform rejects transitions that 6.10 would refuse. PACG 6.10 return codes
  3. Back office: suspend or block only on the grounds of the gaming account contract (fraud or collusion, use by others; operator initiative or an ADM or court request for transparency, security, proper service or force majeure; block after a suspension on seizure or authority order). Mandatory reason from a fixed list mapped to the PACG reasons, a free-text note, user and time in an electronic register; 6.10 is sent when the action is taken; the player (and ADM) is told the reason unless confidentiality is requested. Gaming account contract art. 14; ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.5, 2.9.5.1
  4. A suspension lasts at most 180 days, then the account returns to open unless blocked or closed. Concession agreement art. 17 c. 3 (a); Gaming account contract art. 14; ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.5.1
  5. Withdrawals are allowed by default during a suspension unless the reason forbids it (fraud or collusion investigations, ADM or court orders), and never in the blocked state. The central system refuses movements on accounts suspended with reasons 9, 10, 11 (1471) and on blocked accounts (1271). On a closed account a withdrawal is allowed unless the reason is 9, 10 or 11; a top-up is refused (1203). PACG 6.3 description and codes
  6. When the ID document is no longer valid the account is updated to suspended and the player cannot play, top up or withdraw. Updated document data go to ADM with 6.19; the state is read with 6.11. ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.9.1; PACG 6.19, 6.11

L. Closure and re-registration

  1. "Close my account" in the profile sends 6.10 at once (reason: requested by the holder); the state is closed only on the positive reply. There is no login on a closed account (6.12 returns 1488). Money left on the account, including winnings credited later, stays on it and is paid through customer support on a verified request (claimant's ID and payout instrument checked, PACG withdrawal recorded on the closed account). Late credits of bets open at closure go to the closed account and alert support. ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.13, 2.9.13.1; PACG 6.12
  2. Re-registration with the same credentials only 15 days after closure (the closure date is stored and the tax code blocked). It creates a new account with its own account code, ledger and 6.1 opening; the old account and its history stay separate and unchanged. A closed account is never reopened (1209). PACG 6.10 code 1209
  3. Three rules are enforced by the platform and not by the protocol: the 7-day re-enabling after a revocation request (the protocol has only 1484), the 15-day wait before re-registration, and the 180-day cap on a suspension. ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.4.1, 2.9.5.1; Concession agreement art. 17, 20; Gaming account contract art. 14, 15, 16

Further precisions from the ADM text

  • Re-enabling after a revocation request. The seven days run from the day after the request: with a request on day D, play returns at the start of day D+8. The 270 days count from the day the player is self-excluded. ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.4.1
  • Code for a too-early revocation. 1484 (under 270 days) is the code of 6.16; 1296 "less than six months" belongs to the older message 4.24. PACG 6.16, 4.24, table 7.11
  • Declared balance. V3 has no refusal code for a declared balance that differs from the register, and the reply has no warning channel. The platform sends the correct balance, computed after the movement and including the bonus share. PACG 6.3, 6.5, 6.8 return codes; ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.1.1.1, 2.9.8.1
  • Dormancy. The dormant message 6.14 is sent immediately at the end of three years without movement, counting only bets, wins, refunds, top-ups and withdrawals; the balance sent is net of bonus. The rules are in the task CHG-376993; this kit covers the message. ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.14, 2.9.14.1; PACG 6.14
  • Self-limit periods. Four periods exist, including yearly. PACG table 7.17; ODV guidelines 2025 par. 2.9.1.1
  • Self-exclusion on ADM's public page. The player can request a self-exclusion on ADM's portal with SPID, or through any operator where they have an account. Each operator still offers its own screen. The three types are cross-operator, single operator and single game category.

6. Screens

  • Registration: contract and separate clause approval before submission; final screen with the account code and the unchecked confirmation box; refusal screens for 1200, 1302, 1303, 1304, 1449, 1451, 1452 and 1454.
  • Daily self-limit entry: three limits at registration, four types later, with the first-activation caps and the date each change takes effect.
  • Self-exclusion: choice of variant, category list (sports betting, virtual betting, horse betting, casino), duration 7 to 270 days with the end date shown before confirming, revocation from day 270 with a confirmation step and responsible-gaming information.
  • Account status: the reason of a suspension or block shown to the player unless confidentiality is requested; "Close my account" in the profile.
  • Statement: one itemised account statement in time order.
