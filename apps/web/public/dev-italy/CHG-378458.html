Registration identity: Sumsub KYC and SPID with Namirial

The ADM rules for the two registration routes the partners serve: document plus KYC provider, and SPID digital identity.

Use with the tasks CHG-378458, CHG-378459. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails.

At a glance

  • Two routes. Route B: ID document plus KYC provider (CHG-378458, Sumsub). Route A: SPID or CIE digital identity (CHG-378459, Namirial).
  • Netbet verifies. ADM's positive reply does not replace Netbet's own identity check, at opening and at every change.
  • Personal-data type sent to ADM: 1 manual, 2 SPID, 3 CIE.
  • The opening message 6.1 and its refusals are in CHG-375322.

Rules

A. Both routes

  1. Netbet identifies the player and verifies the identity at opening and at every change: a valid, unexpired ID document matching the data. No vendor or method is prescribed, but the checks must be adequate; ADM's positive reply does not replace them. Guidelines 2.9.9; Technical rules Part 2 ch. 9; Concession agreement Art. 17(2)(b), (d); PACG 6.1; ADM determination 704968 Arts. 1-2
  2. 6.1 carries: unique account code, tax code, surname, name, sex, birth date, municipality and province, residence, document type, number, issue date, issuing authority, place of issue and expiry date, e-mail, nickname, personal-data type, documents acquired, and the three daily limits. Not sent: mobile number, password, document images. PACG 6.1
  3. 6.1 is sent only after the documents are acquired; sent as "not acquired" it is refused (1449). An expired document is refused (1451), an invalid tax code (1304), a minor (1302), an existing account (1200). PACG 6.1, table 7.20
  4. First-activation daily limits: connection time 3 hours, spend 100 EUR, deposits 200 EUR; for players aged 18 to 24, 2 hours, 50 EUR and 50 EUR. A higher value is refused (1454). The amounts offered at registration stay within these caps. Guidelines 2.9.9, 2.9.9.1; Concession agreement Art. 20 c. 3 (g, h); ADM FAQ 12 March 2025; PACG 6.1 code 1454
  5. Identity data, the document copies (route B) or the digital-identity expiry date (route A), top-up and withdrawal records and the connection data are kept for 10 years. Gaming account contract art. 5 c. 20, art. 19 c. 4

B. Route B: document plus KYC provider (Sumsub)

  1. The player enters the contact, nickname, tax code, residence, password, limits and contract acceptance; the provider's approved result supplies the document data: surname, name, sex, date of birth, birth municipality and province, document type, number, issue date, issuing authority, place of issue and expiry date. PACG table 7.15; PACG 6.1
  2. The tax code is checked against name, sex and birth data, and a mismatch is fixed before the account is opened at ADM. The document copies are kept. Personal-data type sent: 1 (manual). PACG table 7.15; Gaming account contract art. 5 c. 20
  3. (Should be checked with the partner: whether Sumsub is supported by the platform, which checks Netbet configures, which fields the approved result returns, and whether it screens PEP and sanctions lists.)

C. Route A: SPID with Namirial

  1. SPID digital identity at security level 2 or higher. The data come from the digital identity: name, surname, tax code, date, municipality, province and country of birth, residence, ID document type, number, issuing authority and expiry date, mobile number, e-mail, expiry date of the digital identity. Anything the identity does not return, the player enters. Guidelines 2.9.9.1; PACG table 7.15
  2. Personal-data type sent to ADM: 2 for SPID (3 for CIE). The player adds nickname, password, the three limits and the contract acceptance; no document upload is needed. PACG table 7.15
  3. Netbet must be able to obtain the ID and tax-code copies from the identity provider and supply them within 10 days of an authority's request. Gaming account contract art. 5 c. 20, art. 19 c. 4
  4. (Should be checked with the partner: whether Namirial returns all the data ADM requires, including the mobile number and the expiry date of the digital identity; the predetermined deposit-limit amounts offered at registration.)
