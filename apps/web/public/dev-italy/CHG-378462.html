Virtual sports expansion with ISIBET

The ADM rules for selling virtual sports through the virtual-betting protocol (PSV 2.0), which apply to any virtual-sports provider.

Use with the task CHG-378462. The task is the requirement. This kit adds reference detail for building and testing it: ADM rules with their sources, flows, messages and checks. If the kit and the task differ, the task prevails; if the task and ADM's protocol differ, the protocol prevails.

At a glance

  • Virtual events are sold under ADM's virtual-betting protocol PSV. The full kit is CHG-375324.
  • Schedules and events are opened at ADM before betting opens; the draw request closes betting; official results, payment and refund follow.
  • Who opens the schedules and events at ADM, requests the random numbers and sends the official results for ISIBET is to be checked with the partner.

Rules

  1. A programme is the daily schedule of one discipline on one virtual platform. It can be opened at most one day ahead of the request date; only one programme per discipline can be coded for the current day. PSV 7, 7.1
  2. Each event is declared to the central system before bets are accepted. The event opening (9000.2) returns the event code and opens bet acceptance at the same moment. PSV 7, 7.2
  3. Only platforms known to ADM can be used: cod_piatt is the unique code ADM assigns to the virtual platform. Unknown platform 2008, blocked platform 2023. PSV 7.1; Guidelines 5.3
  4. The programme, event, draw, results and cancellation messages (service 9000) are enabled for the FSC or CFS client. (Should be checked with the partner: who sends them for ISIBET: the provider through its own Italian integration, a connectivity provider, or Netbet's system.) PSV 5 to 12, enabled client lines
  5. The draw request (9000.3) closes bet acceptance at the same moment and returns the pseudo-random numbers; the request must not come more than eight hours after the event was opened. PSV 7.3; Guidelines 3.3.11.1
  6. After the event the official results are sent (9000.4). If they differ from those calculated by the totalisator (2024), payments on that event are suspended. PSV 7.4, 8.2, 14.16
  7. Settlement has two steps: payment or refund through PSV (9100.2), then the credit through PACG; winnings are credited within one hour of the official certification of the event. PSV 8.2; PACG 7.5, 7.8; Guidelines 2.9.17
  8. All virtual events open at the time of a malfunction are cancelled by the acceptance system (9000.5) and every bet on them is refunded in full. Guidelines 4.1.1; PSV 7.5
  9. Stake and win limits for virtual betting: unit stake EUR 0.50, minimum EUR 1 per ticket, maximum potential win EUR 50,000 per ticket; an event is open at most eight hours. Guidelines 3.3.2, 3.3.3, 3.3.6, 3.3.11

Sources
