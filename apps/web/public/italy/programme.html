Where the scope and the ADM rules meet

  • The SPID flow (item 18) differs from the "classic registration" default in section 3: it starts with the tax code, takes name, e-mail and phone from the identity and sets the deposit limit in step 4. ADM's first-activation caps apply to that limit, and ADM's minimum identity data must come back from Namirial.
  • Sumsub (items 13 and 19) is not among the KYC providers found in the Centrivo Dynamics guide. Whether it also screens PEP and sanctions lists decides how much of the AML list-screening work is covered.
  • Virtual sports through ISIBET (item 22) falls under ADM's virtual-betting protocol. Who opens the schedules and events at ADM for ISIBET is a question for Netbet.
  • Registration field "Politically Exposed Person tick" is marked "No" while item 13 asks for PEP screening. To clarify with Netbet.

Step-by-step details

Steps overlap where a step says so; the numbers show the order in which each one must finish. Betting on the Digitain platform cannot open before certification and a positive technical-functional check in production, where ADM requires it (step 8).

ADM runs its own calendar for the new technical rules: all technical-functional checks are due by 12 February 2027 and certified systems start in production on 15 February 2027 (reported by the trade press quoting ADM; the original is not on ADM's public pages). As we read it, that calendar applies to Netbet's current platform and comes before the steps below. ADM's shortcut for reusing components already in production is a transition measure for old-regime concession holders and ends when the new systems start in production, so plan for a near-full lab verification of the Digitain platform; the lab decides the scope.

  1. 1

    Gap analysis and grooming

    Digitain complianceProduct teamsAbout 2.5 months: Nov 2026 to Jan 2027
    • Every requirement is compared with what the platform already does.
    • Gaps become change tickets. Product teams review them, ask questions, and the tickets are groomed again.
    • Open questions go to Netbet, because ADM leaves some measures to the operator.
    • In the same period, without waiting for the end of grooming: choose the lab and ask for its scope and quote, prepare the Sogei connection and access, and start the draft certification request.

    Exit gate: every requirement has an owner and a status, the product teams have sized the work, and the lab shortlist and the scope question are with the labs.

  2. 2

    Implementation

    Digitain product and tech4.5 to 9 months from February 2027 (placeholder)
    • Build the five protocol adapters, the integrity check, the reconciliation and the missing platform functions. Packages start as soon as they are groomed; the first ones do not wait for the last.
    • Run preliminary tests in Sogei's test environment as soon as access is granted (step 3); the formal sessions are opened by the lab.
    • Prepare what the lab needs: technical report, annotated source code, reproducible builds, list of critical files.

    Low confidence. General guides give 3 to 6 months for a technical integration (not Italian data). No public source gives a build effort for Italy, our gap list is large, and the range may be low if Digitain has no ADM adapters yet, so treat it as a placeholder. Team capacity is not yet known. The product teams must size it.

    Exit gate: every adapter passes its preliminary tests in Sogei's environment and the documents for the lab are complete.

  3. 3

    Early draft and test access

    Netbet filesDigitain suppliesADM procedure, note 706909
    • Netbet enters the Digitain system in ADM's reserved area as a draft certification request. Once it is complete (all mandatory components, no blocking errors), Netbet can ask for Sogei's test environment.
    • Only one test-environment request per concession can be open at a time. Test access comes before the formal request to the lab. The draft can be edited until the test environment is requested; after that it can only be sent or deleted, so complete it first (our reading of ADM's note).

    Exit gate: draft request complete, test environment open and the Sogei connection working.

  4. 4

    Formal submission and lab queue

    Netbet filesDigitain suppliesLab queue: 2 to 8 weeks
    • Netbet sends the request to one of ADM's verification bodies. From then on it can no longer be edited, only withdrawn and re-filed as a copy.
    • Software and documents go to the lab outside ADM's system. The lab is not chosen yet. The queue is the first wait, so the lab should be booked during development.

    Exit gate: the lab has taken the request in charge.

  5. 5

    Lab verification and Sogei protocol tests, in parallel

    Verification bodySogeiDigitainLab testing: 3 to 6 monthsEach Sogei session: 1 working week
    • The lab checks the whole system: source code, documents, every component, the site and apps, communication between components and with ADM, game rules and the random number generator.
    • Each Sogei test session must finish within one Monday to Friday week and pass every point. One failed point means a complete new session. A request the lab rejects is re-filed as a new one.

    The lab uploads its outcome to ADM: a positive one leads to ADM's certificate, a negative one to a rejected request. Time for fixes and repeat sessions is an allowance of ours (section 3).

    Exit gate: the lab reports full conformity to ADM.

  6. 6

    ADM review and certification

    ADMReview time not publishedPlanning allowance: 4 to 12 weeks
    • ADM checks (as reported) that the lab's work is complete and regular, then issues the certification (reported to be sent by certified e-mail; the original instructions are not in hand).
    • The allowance is our own. ADM has published no review time for this step.

    Exit gate: certificate issued.

  7. 7

    Migration preparation, alongside steps 2 to 6

    DigitainNetbetAllowance, included in the totals
    • Plan and rehearse the move of accounts, balances, bonuses, limits, self-exclusions, open bets and late settlement, game history and records, and the reconciliation with ADM's figures.
    • Prepare a rollback and the notice to players. As we read note 706909, only one certified system is in operation at a time and the previous one is archived on the start day, so going back to it would mean a new certification request: prepare that request in advance.
    • Open bets cannot simply be voided: ADM allows annulment only in the cases its game rules provide, such as a malfunction. ADM issues ticket and session IDs and rejects reused transaction IDs, and a payment must match the account of the sale, so, as we read the rules, account numbers and ADM identifiers should carry over unchanged and numbering ranges must not overlap on the day.
    • One balance per player, loaded with its bonus split, limits, self-exclusion dates and payment instruments. All players log in again on the new system, which re-checks their state at ADM.
    • Only the last rehearsal on the certified build and the cut-over itself sit after certification.
    • Any pause in betting needs ADM's authorisation: an unauthorised suspension of collection is penalised and, beyond 30 consecutive days, can lead to forfeiture of the concession (concession Art. 15(1)).

    The ADM texts we hold set no rule for the move itself, so the allowances are ours and the lab and ADM should be asked how they want open bets and records handled.

    Exit gate: a rehearsal on the certified build reconciles with ADM's figures, the rollback is tested and the cut-over window is agreed.

  8. 8

    Start date and ADM production check

    ADMNetbetDigitainADM gives at least 5 days' notice
    • When the lab's result is positive, Netbet declares the start date in the reserved area; ADM issues its certificate in parallel. On that day the new system goes into operation and the previous one is archived.
    • As reported by the trade press, the check is done in the live environment, once per game type: sales, bet acceptance, accounting, crediting of wins, payments and messages to ADM. Up to five test accounts held by a company can be opened for it (ADM account-register protocol; two older messages say ten, to confirm with Sogei). Trade press reports that schedules are published beforehand.
    • ADM sets the date and gives at least 5 days' notice. That notice is a rule; the 2-week allowance in section 3 is ours and separate from it. ADM takes these checks in the order requests arrive.
    • Faults found are fixed before a repeat check.

    Trade press reports that ADM ties this check to the first certification, at the first start of each game type. Whether it repeats for a platform migration is ADM's call: the concession lets ADM repeat checks after technology changes, so plan for it.

    Exit gate: start date declared and, where ADM requires it, a positive production check. Bet collection starts only after that.

  9. 9

    Go-live and changes afterwards

    NetbetDigitain
    • The system must run exactly as certified.
    • A change to a critical file or a new function goes to the lab first. Releases of such changes wait for its answer.
    • The Technical Rules set no day limit for urgent fixes. The route and its timing should be agreed with the lab in advance.
  10. +

    Parallel Netbet track: reports and responsible-gaming measures

    NetbetADMReview time not published
    • Netbet must re-file its updated technical report to ADM after the technology change (the concession requires it) and, where the tools change, resubmits its responsible-gaming measures, which ADM assesses in advance.
    • Digitain supplies the descriptions of its functions. Planning assumption: ready before step 4; ADM may ask for additions within ten days.

    Whether the migration needs a fresh ADM assessment of the responsible-gaming measures is still to be confirmed with Netbet.

4. What could move the dates, and what is still open

What could add or save time

Could add time

  • A failed Sogei point restarts the whole session, and a rejected request is filed again.
  • A failed lab test or maths model adds weeks and a new submission.
  • Many operators filing before ADM's February 2027 deadline, which lengthens the lab and ADM queues.
  • Game providers each use their own lab, and shared components must be certified first.
  • Rules ADM may still issue, such as hetero-exclusion and simplified technical-functional checks.
  • Open scope questions: who provides the virtual sports platform, and whether casino games still need ADM's per-game authorisation.
  • A migration that does not reconcile with ADM's figures in rehearsal.

Could save time

  • Booking the lab before development ends.
  • Complete documents on day one, so the lab does not send the submission back.
  • Running preliminary Sogei tests as each adapter is ready, instead of at the end.
  • Using one lab for both the platform and Netbet's system: trade press reports that ADM gives such cases priority. To confirm with the lab.

To confirm before these dates go on a plan

  • Development effort and team capacity per work package, sized by the product teams, with one named owner per package.
  • Which verification body Netbet will use, and its real queue, slot and quote.
  • How long ADM takes to review a lab result, and to assess responsible-gaming measures. Ask ADM or the lab.
  • How wide the lab's verification of the Digitain platform will be. ADM handles a platform change as a change request and the lab sets the scope, so a near-full verification is the safe planning case. Also whether the production check repeats for a migration, and whether a game platform already certified for another concessionaire needs less work: ADM's transition shortcut does not cover that case after production start.
  • Whether the Digitain sportsbook is already in ADM's library of certified platforms, which lab certified it, and how ADM authorises Digitain as producer. ADM certifies each concessionaire's own system, so if the platform is not in the library, Netbet's certification has to cover the platform itself, not only its integration.
  • The migration scope and rules: how ADM and the lab want open bets, balances, bonuses, limits and records handled at cut-over, how many rehearsals, and the rollback criteria.

Shared sportsbook and Netbet's own account system

Digitain's sportsbook is a standalone platform joined to the account system (PAM) by an API, and one instance serves several operators. The account system is separate per operator.

What ADM's texts say

  • The gaming account system holds only the concessionaire's data. A database or schema shared between concessionaires is refused.
  • The data of each game system, accounting included, is separated at least logically per concessionaire.
  • ADM certifies the concessionaire's whole system and lists each component with its manufacturer.

Our plan, not yet confirmed

  • Netbet certifies one system: its own isolated account system plus the Digitain sportsbook as a component, Digitain named as manufacturer.
  • Netbet's bets, tickets and accounting in the sportsbook are separated logically per concessionaire.
  • Every message to ADM goes under Netbet's concession code.

To check Whether ADM and the lab accept this, whether a supplier can certify its sportsbook once under its own name, and how ADM authorises a manufacturer. The question is with the consultant list. No ADM text answers it.
