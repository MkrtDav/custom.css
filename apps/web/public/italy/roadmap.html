Netbet Italy · Digitain platform · stakeholder briefing · 18 November 2026

From regulation to go-live

For the product, technology, compliance and Netbet teams: what ADM requires of the platform, the steps between a finished build and live play, and a realistic calendar. Netbet already operates in Italy on its current provider, so no licence deadline drives this plan. The pace is set by development and by waits outside our control.

2. The path to go-live

Each step starts only after the one before it, except where a step says it runs in parallel. Betting cannot open before step 6 is complete.

  1. 1

    Gap analysis and grooming

    Digitain complianceProduct teamsAbout 2.5 months: Nov 2026 to Jan 2027
    • Every requirement is compared with what the platform already does.
    • Gaps become change tickets. Product teams review them, ask questions, and the tickets are groomed again.
    • Open questions go to Netbet, because ADM leaves some measures to the operator.
  2. 2

    Implementation

    Digitain product and tech4.5 to 9 months from February 2027
    • Build the five protocol adapters, the integrity check, the reconciliation and the missing platform functions.
    • Test against Sogei's test environment, which Sogei makes available before the certification request is filed.
    • Prepare what the lab needs: technical report, annotated source code, reproducible builds, list of critical files.

    Low confidence. Industry sources give 3 to 6 months for the technical integration and 18 to 24 weeks for a vendor build, and our gap list is large, so the range runs higher. The product teams must size it.

  3. 3

    Certification request and lab selection

    Netbet filesDigitain suppliesLab queue: 2 to 8 weeks
    • Netbet chooses one of ADM's verification bodies and files the request in ADM's reserved area.
    • The lab is not chosen yet. The queue is the first wait, so the lab should be booked during development.
  4. 4

    Lab verification and Sogei protocol tests, in parallel

    Verification bodySogeiDigitainLab testing: 3 to 6 monthsEach Sogei session: 1 working week
    • The lab checks the whole system: source code, documents, every component, the site and apps, communication between components and with ADM, game rules and the random number generator.
    • Each Sogei test session must finish within one Monday to Friday week and pass every point. One failed point means a complete new session.

    The lab sends its result to ADM only when the whole system has passed.

  5. 5

    ADM review and certification

    ADMReview time not publishedPlanning allowance: 4 to 12 weeks
    • ADM checks that the lab's work is complete and regular, then issues the certification by certified e-mail.
    • The allowance comes from other regulators. ADM has published no figure for this step.
  6. 6

    ADM production check

    ADMNetbetDigitainAt least 5 days' notice
    • Done in the live environment, once per game type: sales, bet acceptance, accounting, crediting of wins, payments and messages to ADM.
    • Five test accounts held by a company are registered and their schedules published beforehand.
    • Faults found are fixed before a repeat check.

    Bet collection may start only after certification and a positive production check. Whether the check repeats for a platform migration is still to be confirmed.

  7. 7

    Go-live and changes afterwards

    NetbetDigitain
    • The system must run exactly as certified.
    • A change to a critical file or a new function goes to the lab first. Releases of such changes wait for its answer.
    • Secondary sources report that an emergency fix may go out first, provided the lab certifies it within two weeks. To be confirmed.
  8. +

    Parallel Netbet track: reports and responsible-gaming measures

    NetbetADMReview time not published
    • Netbet updates its technical report to ADM and, where the tools change, resubmits its responsible-gaming measures, which ADM assesses in advance.
    • Digitain supplies the descriptions of its functions. This must be ready before step 3.

    Whether the migration needs a fresh ADM assessment of the responsible-gaming measures is still to be confirmed with Netbet.

Booking the lab during development can take the queue off the critical path, which would bring each date forward by up to two months. Lab queues may also be longer than the averages if many operators file at the same time. That is our reading, not an ADM statement.

4. What could move the dates, and what is still open

Could add time

  • A failed Sogei point restarts the whole session.
  • A failed lab test or maths model adds weeks and a new submission.
  • Game providers each use their own lab, and shared components must be certified first.
  • Rules still to be issued by ADM, such as the provision on accepted digital ID tools.
  • Open scope questions: who provides the virtual sports platform, and whether casino games still need ADM's per-game authorisation.

Could save time

  • Components already certified in Italy, such as providers' game platforms, need only an integration check.
  • Booking the lab before development ends.
  • Complete documents on day one, so the lab does not send the submission back.
  • Starting the Sogei protocol tests as each adapter is ready, instead of at the end.

To confirm before these dates go on a plan

  • Development effort, sized by the product teams.
  • Which verification body Netbet will use, and its real queue and quote.
  • How long ADM takes to review a lab result, and to assess responsible-gaming measures. Ask ADM or the lab.
  • Whether the Digitain migration is handled as a new certification or as a change to Netbet's certified system, and whether the production check repeats.
