The whole picture

What the current platform diagram does not contain

The architecture document lists 22 service groups, plus the database, message broker, cache and external providers, and no component that talks to ADM. The five protocol adapters, the integrity-check service, the accounting and reconciliation service and a common time source are therefore new work, or must be shown to exist elsewhere in the platform. Hosting is already in the EU, which matches the rule that the infrastructure sits in the European Economic Area.
